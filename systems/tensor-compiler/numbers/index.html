32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — Node Overhead Budget. 5,000,000 nodes × 256 B/node = 1.28 GB IR overhead, inside the 8 GB §4 default ceiling, leaving 6.72 GB for buffers, arenas, and pass working sets.
  • C2 — Cold Parse Time. 5,000,000 nodes ÷ 100,000 nodes/sec = 50 sec worst-case cold parse, exceeding the p99 20 sec compile-latency budget (§4) ⇒ the largest graphs require streaming/incremental parse (§13).
  • C3 — Pass Budget Per Node. 10,000 transforms/sec × 20 sec p99 window = 200,000 transformations available, and 200,000 ÷ 5,000,000 nodes = 0.04 average transformations-per-node, forcing selective/greedy rewrite scheduling (§16) rather than exhaustive fixpoint iteration.
  • C4 — Memory-Plan Reuse Gain. 40% peak-reduction target (§17) applied to an illustrative 3.2 GB unplanned peak = 1.28 GB saved, landing at 1.92 GB peak, inside an 8 GB GPU's 80% VRAM cap (6.4 GB, §4).
  • C5 — Launch-Storm Ceiling. 4,096 max streams (§4) × 20 µs launch overhead (§4) = 81.92 ms worst-case fully-serialized launch storm, which async queue overlap (§20) must hide to keep the p95 20 µs per-op dispatch target intact.
  • C6 — FD Budget Per Worker. 8,192 max FDs (§4) ÷ 128 concurrent compilation workers (§4) = 64 FDs/worker average, covering artifact files, mmap segments, and telemetry sockets.
  • C7 — Max Artifact Size. 5,000,000 nodes × 256 B/node × 2.5x amplification ceiling (§4) = 3.2 GB maximum artifact-bundle size for the largest supported graph.
  • C8 — Composite Availability. 99.5% compiler availability × 99.95% runtime availability (§4) = 99.45% end-to-end compile-then-execute availability, ≈ 2,890 minutes/year combined downtime budget.
  • C9 — Worst-Case Recovery Chain. 30 sec compiler-crash RTO + 5 sec runtime-restart RTO + 60 sec replay-reconstruction RTO (§4) = 95 sec worst-case full recovery chain, inside a 5-minute operator escalation SLA.
  • C10 — Lock-Wait Ceiling. 2% lock-contention overhead ceiling (§4) × 5 µs p50 dispatch (§4) = 100 ns max amortized lock-wait per dispatch call, across up to 4,096 concurrent streams.
  • C11 — NUMA Arena Sizing. 128 compilation workers (§4) ÷ 8 NUMA nodes on a representative dual-socket host = 16 workers/node, and 8 GB ÷ 8 nodes = 1 GB max per-node arena size to avoid cross-node spill.
  • C12 — Replay Ring-Buffer Sizing. 10,000 transforms/sec optimizer ceiling (§4) × 60 sec replay-reconstruction window (§4) = 600,000 events, and 600,000 × 64 B/event = 38.4 MB bounded ring buffer per session, well inside the 8 GB memory ceiling.
