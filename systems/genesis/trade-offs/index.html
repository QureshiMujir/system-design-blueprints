36. Alternative Architectures (Roads Not Taken)

(a) LLM-proposal search, à la FunSearch, instead of syntactic mutation.

  seed program(s)
       |
       v
  +-----------+      propose      +------------+
  |    LLM    | ----------------> | candidate  |
  | (frozen)  |                   |  program   |
  +-----------+                   +-----+------+
       ^                                |
       | prompt = best-scoring          v
       | prior candidates        +------------+
       +------------------------ | evaluator  |
                                  | (sandbox)  |
                                  +------------+

Winning regime: open-ended mathematical/algorithmic discovery where the space of "reasonable next edits" is not well captured by tree-shaped syntactic mutation — an LLM's prior over plausible code is a stronger proposal distribution than random typed-subtree search. Real embodying system: FunSearch (Romera-Paredes et al. 2024). Why rejected here: Genesis targets P1's bit-for-bit determinism and P4's content-addressed reproducibility; an LLM proposal distribution is neither deterministic nor practically reproducible run-to-run without pinning a specific model checkpoint indefinitely — tracked as a deferral (PhaseDeferred, §37), not a rejection of the idea's value.

(b) Gradient-based / differentiable program synthesis.

  program template (differentiable relaxation)
              |
              v
   +--------------------+
   | continuous params  | <--- gradient step
   +----------+---------+
              |
              v
   +--------------------+
   |  loss(program(x))  |
   +--------------------+

Winning regime: domains where the target program has a natural differentiable relaxation (e.g. neural-network-shaped search spaces, soft program sketches) and gradient signal is far cheaper per step than a full sandboxed evaluation. Real embodying system: differentiable program synthesis lines of work stemming from neural module networks and program-relaxation research. Why rejected here: Genesis's typed AST IR (§8) and .gns fitness DSL (§14) are discrete by construction — control flow (If), variable binding (Let), and typed primitive calls do not have a natural differentiable relaxation without abandoning the closure-typed representation P5 depends on.

(c) SMT-guided enumerative synthesis.

  spec (from .gns, expressed as constraints)
              |
              v
   +--------------------+      UNSAT / model
   |   SMT solver        | <--------------------+
   +----------+----------+                      |
              | candidate program                |
              v                                  |
   +--------------------+   counterexample  -----+
   |  verify vs spec     | ------------------------
   +--------------------+

Winning regime: small, tightly bounded program spaces with a precise logical specification, where exhaustive-but-guided search (CEGIS-style counterexample loops) can prove optimality or unsatisfiability, not just find a good-enough candidate. Real embodying system: Sketch, Rosette, and CEGIS-family enumerative synthesizers. Why rejected here: the .gns DSL's case-based fitness (§8) is deliberately example-driven and tolerant of approximate/partial matches (weighted objectives, tolerance), not a decidable logical spec — encoding Genesis's fitness landscape into an SMT-checkable constraint set would discard exactly the soft, weighted-objective expressiveness §14 is designed to provide, and would not scale to the 200,000-genome/generation population Genesis targets (§4).

(d) A MAP-Elites quality-diversity archive replacing scalar fitness ranking entirely.

  population --------> behavior descriptor --------> archive grid
                                                       (no scalar
                                                        ranking at
                                                        all — only
                                                        per-cell
                                                        elitism)

Winning regime: research questions where behavioral diversity is the primary object of study and scalar fitness is actively misleading (deceptive landscapes), making a pure quality-diversity search preferable to any hybrid. Real embodying system: pure MAP-Elites deployments and QDpy-based research pipelines with no scalar-ranking fallback. Why rejected here as the sole mechanism (though adopted as a coexisting mechanism in §20): Genesis's target workloads (automated repair, architecture search) have a genuine external objective (patch correctness, model accuracy) that operators need ranked, not just illuminated — P7 (§6) explicitly keeps scalar-fitness ranking alongside the archive rather than replacing it, because a pure quality-diversity mode would leave operators unable to answer "which genome should I ship" without an additional ad hoc ranking step layered back on top.
