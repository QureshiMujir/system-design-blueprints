32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — Per-hart state size. 32 GPRs × 8 B = 256 B + CSR sparse map (≈200 entries × 16 B) = 3.2 KB + TLB (64 entries × 24 B) = 1.5 KB + PMP (16 × 16 B) = 256 B + trap/misc ≈ 1 KB → total ≈ 6.2 KB, well inside the §4 256 KB per-hart ceiling with ~40x headroom for a richer CSR footprint.
  • C2 — Guest-RAM metadata overhead (1 GB guest). Dirty bitmap 1 GB / 4 KiB page / 8 bits = 32 KB + Sv39 page-table shadow ≈ 2 MB + worst-case dirty-snapshot staging (10% of guest) = 100 MB → 32 KB + 2 MB + 100 MB ≈ 102 MB, inside the §4 200 MB ceiling.
  • C3 — Replay-buffer fill time. ReplayEvent ≈ 23 B header + 18 B register deltas + 17 B memory delta + 8.5 B CSR delta + 4 B interrupt state + 8 B timestamp = 80 B per event, and 80 B × 80 M events/s (§4 MIPS ceiling) = 6.4 GB/s raw, so the 512 MB §4 replay-buffer ceiling fills in 512 MB / 6.4 GB/s ≈ 80 ms without a flush — why zstd compression (ADR-06) and a sub-100 ms flush cadence are load-bearing, not optional.
  • C4 — Boot-trace storage (uncompressed). 300 M instructions (§4 Linux-boot-time derivation) × 80 B/event = 24 GB raw, and at a 4:1 zstd ratio (ADR-06) 24 GB / 4 = 6 GB compressed per full boot trace — why fixtures/replay/replay_boot.trace (§19/§20) is captured once as a golden fixture rather than regenerated per CI run.
  • C5 — FD-budget accounting. 16 trace outputs + 32 snapshot files + 64 device backing files = 112, leaving 256 − 112 = 144 FDs of headroom for GDB/CLI sockets and ephemeral fixture handles under the §4 256-FD ceiling.
  • C6 — Lock-order audit surface. 5 hierarchy levels (I5) × 4 harts × 6 device-class instances (UART/CLINT/PLIC/VirtIO-blk + 2 reserved) = 120 distinct acquisition contexts to audit per subsystem change — small enough for the single-reviewer discipline P3/P11 assume.
  • C7 — Snapshot restore time budget. Header deserialize (negligible) + blake3 hash over up to 100 MB dirty-staging body (C2) at ≈1 GB/s = 100 ms + quiesce release (negligible) ≈ 100 ms total, well inside the 30 s §4 RTO, leaving > 290x margin for a slower storage backend.
  • C8 — Conformance-suite CI cost. ≈1,200 decode vectors (§13.4) + ≈400 privileged vectors (§17.4) = 1,600 vectors, and 1,600 × ≈5 ms average (decode + execute + Spike differential) = 8 s total conformance run time, cheap enough to gate every PR rather than running nightly only.
  • C9 — Availability-budget alert threshold. 99.5% crash-free target (§4) over a 1,000-run nightly CI farm allows at most 1,000 × 0.5% = 5 crashing runs before the SLO is breached, setting the alert threshold at 6 in the replay-farm dashboard (§24).
  • C10 — Dispatch-latency headroom. 1 / 80 MIPS = 12.5 ns theoretical minimum dispatch interval, and the §4 35 ns p50 budget / 12.5 ns = 2.8x that floor — the headroom the decode-table cache-locality work (§13.7) is meant to preserve as instruction mixes shift toward CSR-heavy kernel code.
