Phase 6 built 2026-10-06 (bounded-load consistent hashing, §18): chr-ring's BoundedRing (Mirrokni et al.) caps every node at ceil(c * total / n) placements (c = 1.25 default, any finite c >= 1.0; below, NaN or infinite -> BadWeight) by checking the key being placed against ceil(c * (m + 1) / n), computed exactly on the binary value of c, and forwarding a full successor's key clockwise to the next non-full node; the walk is one lap with a circuit-breaker (CapacityExceeded once every member is full). place/account charge, release(node) gives back, Placement::locate/locate_n read under the current loads (locate_n = the zone-aware preference walk over the non-full nodes); ForwardStats, DEFAULT_CAPACITY_FACTOR. ⬜ Phase 12 — Concurrency and production embedding §24

Objectives
  • Wrap any Placement impl in a lock-free-read snapshot using arc-swap; zero allocation on locate/locate_n hot path.
