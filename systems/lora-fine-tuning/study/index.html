34. Mental Models & First Principles

  • MM1 — "A LoRA adapter is a rank-constrained gradient sketch, not a smaller model." Predicts why merging is lossless and why rank choice trades expressivity for parameter count. Bug class without it: treating adapters as independently-trainable mini-networks expected to generalize without the frozen base. Applies in §8 (Domain Model), §23 (Phase 11 merge).
  • MM2 — "Zero-init B makes the adapter a no-op at step 0, not an approximation of one." Predicts step-0 forward equivalence is exact (I3), not approximate. Bug class: initializing both A and B nonzero and being surprised training starts from a perturbed model. Applies in §11.3 (Core Schema), §12 (Phase 0).
  • MM3 — "The optimizer only sees what requires_grad exposes it to." Predicts that freezing is a graph-construction-time decision, not a training-time flag. Bug class: freezing base params after building the optimizer, leaking stale param groups (I2). Applies in §7 (I1/I2), §12 (Phase 0).
  • MM4 — "Quantization and adaptation are orthogonal axes." Predicts QLoRA's forward composes cleanly (dequantize base, then add the adapter delta) without special-casing. Bug class: writing adapter-quantization-aware code paths instead of composing two independent transforms. Applies in §6 (P14), §19 (Phase 7).
  • MM5 — "The chat template is part of the model's contract, not a serving convenience." Predicts template/adapter coupling is load-bearing and that decoupling them is a deployment bug waiting to happen. Bug class: assuming any "close enough" template works at inference. Applies in §6 (P3), §7 (I5), §15 (Phase 3).
  • MM6 — "Importance-score pruning is regret minimization under a rank budget." Predicts AdaLoRA's monotonic-shrink behavior and why growing rank mid-run breaks the metaphor. Bug class: expecting AdaLoRA to reallocate rank upward to a module that later needs more. Applies in §7 (I11), §20 (Phase 8).
  • MM7 — "Magnitude and direction are separable levers on the same weight." Predicts why DoRA closes the LoRA-vs-full-fine-tune gap where plain LoRA cannot: full fine-tuning moves both magnitude and direction, LoRA moves only direction implicitly through BA. Bug class: expecting plain LoRA to match full-fine-tune quality on magnitude-dominated tasks. Applies in §21 (Phase 9).
  • MM8 — "Multi-adapter serving is a scheduling problem wearing a machine-learning costume." Predicts that hot-swap latency, isolation, and VRAM budgeting are the same problems as connection pooling and tenant isolation in any multi-tenant system. Bug class: under-investing in AdapterStore's concurrency correctness because "it's just ML weights." Applies in §7 (I7/I14), §24 (Phase 12).

  • **Raschka. Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch), ch. 5 (pretraining) and ch. 6/7 (fine-tuning, instruction-tuning).** Applied in §15 (dataset/chat-template phase) and §12 (Phase 0 correctness kernel).
  • Tunstall, von Werra, Wolf. Natural Language Processing with Transformers, ch. 4 (transfer learning), ch. 10 (efficient transformers). Applied in §14 (model preparation) and §11 (Core Schema).
  • Lin, Rush, Sanh (eds. contributions) — HuggingFace NLP Course, "Fine-tuning" and "Sharing models" units. Applied in §17 (checkpoint/reproducibility) and §29 (Compatibility & Versioning).
  • Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville. Deep Learning, ch. 8 (Optimization, section 8.7 on batch/layer norm) and ch. 15 (Representation Learning). Applied in §21 (DoRA magnitude/direction, which is a representation-learning decomposition) and §8 (Domain Model).
  • Jurafsky & Martin. Speech and Language Processing (3rd ed. draft), ch. 11 (fine-tuning and masked LM) and ch. 12 (prompting). Applied in §22 (prompt/prefix-tuning phase) and §5 papers below (Lester et al., Li & Liang).

Papers

  • Hu, Shen, Wallis, Allen-Zhu, Li, Wang, Wang, Chen. "LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation of Large Language Models." ICLR 2022. Contribution: the W + (α/r)BA decomposition and the α/r-scaling argument. Applied in §8 (Domain Model), §13 (Phase 1), ADR-02/03 (§9).
  • Houlsby, Giurgiu, Jastrzebski, Morrone, de Laroussilhe, Gesmundo, Attariyan, Gelly. "Parameter-Efficient Transfer Learning for NLP." ICML 2019. Contribution: bottleneck adapters, the structural ancestor of LoRA. Applied in §11 (bottleneck.py in Repository Layout, §10) and §2 (Why Build This).
  • Lester, Al-Rfou, Constant. "The Power of Scale for Parameter-Efficient Prompt Tuning." EMNLP 2021. Contribution: soft-prompt tuning and its scale-dependence. Applied in §22 (Phase 10) and ADR table row on prompt-tuning scope (§9).
  • Li, Liang. "Prefix-Tuning: Optimizing Continuous Prompts for Generation." ACL 2021. Contribution: per-layer KV-cache prefixes via an MLP reparameterization. Applied in §22 (Phase 10, PrefixEncoder) and §11 (Core Schema §11.5).
  • Liu, Tam, Muqeeth, Mohta, Huang, Bansal, Raffel. "Few-Shot Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning is Better and Cheaper than In-Context Learning." NeurIPS 2022. Contribution: IA3's element-wise scaling vectors. Applied in §22 (Phase 10, IA3Linear) and §8 (Domain Model).
  • Zhang, Chen, Bukharin, He, Cheng, Chen, Zhao. "AdaLoRA: Adaptive Budget Allocation for Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning." ICLR 2023. Contribution: SVD-form reparameterization P·diag(E)·Q and importance-score-driven rank pruning. Applied in §20 (Phase 8), MM6 (§34).
  • Liu, Wang, Dao, Kang, Panda, Karypis, et al. "DoRA: Weight-Decomposed Low-Rank Adaptation." ICML 2024. Contribution: magnitude/direction decomposition applied to LoRA. Applied in §21 (Phase 9), MM7 (§34), ADR-09 (§9).
  • Dettmers, Pagnoni, Holtzman, Zettlemoyer. "QLoRA: Efficient Finetuning of Quantized LLMs." NeurIPS 2023. Contribution: NF4 quantization, double quantization, paged optimizers. Applied in §19 (Phase 7), §4 (System Constraints VRAM ceilings), §36 (#5).
  • Dettmers, Lewis, Belkada, Zettlemoyer. "LLM.int8(): 8-bit Matrix Multiplication for Transformers at Scale." NeurIPS 2022. Contribution: outlier-aware 8-bit quantization, the predecessor argument to NF4. Applied in §19 (Phase 7 N.2, contrast with NF4).
  • Ding, Qin, Yang, Wei, Yang, Su, Hu, Chen, Chan, Chen, Yi, Zhao, Wang, Chen, Sun, Liu. "Delta Tuning: A Comprehensive Study of Parameter-Efficient Methods for Pre-Trained Language Models." Nature Machine Intelligence, 2023. Contribution: unifying taxonomy (additive, selective, reparameterization). Applied in §8 (Domain Model classification) and §5 reading order (read before individual method papers).

Reference Implementations

  • HuggingFace PEFT (huggingface/peft). Study src/peft/tuners/lora/layer.py (canonical LoraLinear), src/peft/peft_model.py (wrapper composition pattern), src/peft/utils/ save_and_load.py (checkpoint format). Lesson: adapter-as-module-wrapper composition. Pitfall not to copy: PEFT's tuner-class explosion (one class per method with duplicated merge logic) — this blueprint centralizes merge math in merge/merge.py (§10) instead. Applied in §13/§20/§21/§22 (adapter phases) and §23 (Phase 11 merge).
  • Microsoft LoRA reference (microsoft/LoRA). Study the original loralib/layers.py. Lesson: the minimal, unencumbered reference the paper's own scaling/init choices came from. Applied in §13 (Phase 1, cross-check against LoraConfig defaults, §11).
  • bitsandbytes (TimDettmers/bitsandbytes). Study csrc/kernels.cu (NF4 lookup table, dequantization kernel) and the paged-optimizer Python wrapper. Lesson: block-wise quantization with a lookup table is the systems core of QLoRA. Pitfall not to copy: the library's CPU fallback path silently degrades throughput without a loud warning — Phase 7 (§19) makes this an explicit telemetry counter (FM-10, §27). Applied in §19 (Phase 7).
  • Axolotl (axolotl-ai-cloud/axolotl). Study src/axolotl/utils/models.py (model-prep pipeline) and src/axolotl/prompt_tokenizers.py (dataset-formatting patterns). Lesson: a production recipe-driven fine-tuning stack's config-surface design. Applied in §14 (Phase 2) and §9 (ADR-18, recipe format).
  • Unsloth (unslothai/unsloth). Study the fused Triton LoRA forward/backward kernels. Lesson: kernel fusion of the base GEMM and the low-rank delta into one Triton kernel avoids a second memory round-trip. Pitfall not to copy: over-fusing across adapter types (AdaLoRA/DoRA have different forward shapes) — Phase 13 (§25) fuses per-method, not generically. Applied in §25 (Phase 13) and §35 (Comparative Architecture Table).

RFCs / Specifications

  • safetensors format specification (HuggingFace). The adapter checkpoint wire format; read for the header layout. Applied in §17 (Phase 5) and ADR-05 (§9).
  • HuggingFace transformers chat-template documentation (Jinja2-based). The template format chat_templates/registry.py (§10) is byte-compatible with. Applied in §15 (Phase 3) and ADR-10 (§9).

Glossary (§40) · 46 terms · site-wide glossary

Adapter
a small set of trainable parameters wrapping a frozen base layer.
Adapter-only checkpoint
a saved artifact containing only adapter tensors + metadata, not base weights (P9).
AdapterMeta
the per-adapter metadata record (§11.10).
AdapterStore
the GPU-resident, hot-swappable table of mounted adapters (§11.10, §24).
AdaLoRA
a PEFT method reparameterizing BA as an SVD form with importance-driven rank pruning (Zhang et al. 2023).
Alpha (α)
the LoRA scaling numerator; combined with rank to form the scaling factor.
Base model
the frozen, pretrained model an adapter wraps.
bitsandbytes
the native library supplying NF4 quantization and paged optimizers.
Block-wise quantization
quantizing weights in fixed-size blocks (e.g. 64 elements) with a per-block scale constant.
Chat template
a named Jinja-format function rendering a conversation to tokens+labels.
Checkpoint hash / recipe hash
a SHA-256 of the canonical recipe YAML, stamped for reproducibility (I12).
Conformance fixture
a named input/expected-output pair asserting one invariant or acceptance criterion.
Double quantization
quantizing the quantization constants themselves, reducing QLoRA's per-parameter overhead.
DoRA
Weight-Decomposed LoRA; splits a weight into magnitude and direction, adapting direction via LoRA (Liu et al. 2024).
Fan-in/fan-out (fan_in_fan_out)
a flag for Conv1D-style layers (GPT-2) whose weight axes are transposed relative to nn.Linear.
FSDP
Fully Sharded Data Parallel, a PyTorch-native distributed training strategy.
Grouped-query attention (GQA)
an attention variant with fewer key/value heads than query heads, reducing KV-projection parameter count.
Gradient accumulation
summing gradients across micro-batches before an optimizer step, to simulate a larger effective batch.
Gradient checkpointing
trading recomputation for memory by not storing all activations for backward.
Hot-swap
serving a request against a dynamically selected adapter without merging it into the base.
IA3
Infused Adapter by Inhibiting and Amplifying Inner Activations; three learned per-channel scale vectors (Liu et al. 2022).
Injection point
the specific linear layer (e.g. q_proj) an adapter wraps.
Invariant
a property (I#) that must hold at all times, enforced and telemetered (§7).
Label mask
the -100-valued positions in a training example's labels, excluded from loss.
LoRA
Low-Rank Adaptation; the W+ (α/r)BA decomposition (Hu et al. 2021).
Merge
folding an adapter's delta into the base weight, producing a dense, adapter-free model (P5).
Mixed precision
training with a lower-precision compute dtype (bf16/fp16) and higher-precision accumulation.
Multi-adapter serving
one resident base model serving many adapters concurrently (§24).
NF4
4-bit NormalFloat, an information-theoretically optimal 4-bit code for normally-distributed weights (Dettmers et al. 2023).
Orthogonality regularizer
AdaLoRA's auxiliary loss term encouraging P/Q orthogonality.
Paged optimizer
an optimizer whose state pages out to CPU RAM under GPU memory pressure.
PEFT
Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning, the umbrella term for LoRA/AdaLoRA/DoRA/IA3/ prompt/prefix methods.
Perplexity
the exponentiated average negative log-likelihood, an eval metric.
PiSSA
Principal Singular values and Singular vectors Adaptation, an alternative LoRA init from the base weight's SVD.
Prefix-tuning
prepending learned per-layer KV-cache prefixes via a reparameterization MLP (Li & Liang 2021).
Prompt-tuning
prepending learned soft-token embeddings to the input (Lester et al. 2021).
Rank (r)
the inner dimension of the low-rank decomposition BA.
Recipe
a YAML file fully specifying a training run (ADR-18).
Reparameterization trick
generating a prefix via an MLP rather than optimizing it directly, for training stability.
rsLoRA
rank-stabilized LoRA, using α/√r scaling instead of α/r.
safetensors
a checkpoint format with no arbitrary code execution at load.
Scaling factor (s)
the multiplier (α/r or α/√r) applied to the adapter's output.
Sequence packing
concatenating short examples to fill the context length for throughput.
SFT
Supervised Fine-Tuning, training on labeled instruction/response pairs.
Target module
a named linear layer eligible for adapter injection (e.g. q_proj, v_proj).
Triton
a Python-embedded language for writing fused GPU kernels, used for Phase 13's fused LoRA kernels. ---
