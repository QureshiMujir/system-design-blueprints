36. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. Trainable params, r=8 q+v, 8B-class base (32 layers, hidden 4096, GQA kv-dim 1024). Formula: r·(in_q+out_q+in_v+out_v)·layers = 8·(4096+4096+4096+1024)·32 = 8·13,312·32 = 3,407,872 ≈ 3.4 M — 0.043% of an 8 B base (chained from §4's parameter-range row).
  2. Adapter checkpoint size, bf16. 3.4 M params × 2 bytes = 6.8 MB — within §4's 1–100 MB band, at the low end (chained from #1).
  3. Optimizer state size, AdamW fp32 (adapter-only). 3.4 M × 2 (momentum+variance) × 4 bytes = 27.2 MB — negligible next to the base model's 16 GB (chained from #1).
  4. VRAM, LoRA bf16 training, 8B base, batch 4 × seq 2048, grad-checkpointed. Base weights: 8×10⁹ × 2 bytes = 16 GB. Adapter+optimizer: 6.8 MB + 27.2 MB ≈ 34 MB (negligible). Activations with checkpointing: ≈ 6–8 GB at this batch/seq. Total ≈ 22–24 GB — fits §4's ≤24 GB single-consumer-GPU ceiling (chained from #1, #3, §4).
  5. VRAM, QLoRA NF4, 70B base, batch 1 × seq 1024. Base: 70×10⁹ × 0.5 byte = 35 GB. Double-quant constant overhead: 70×10⁹ × 0.127 bit / 8 ≈ 1.1 GB. Adapters+optimizer: tens of MB (negligible at this scale too). Activations with checkpointing: ≈ 8–10 GB at batch 1/seq 1024. Total ≈ 45–46 GB — fits §4's ≤48 GB single-GPU ceiling (chained from §4's double-quantization row).
  6. Max concurrent adapters, H100 80GB, r=8 q,v/8B base. Theoretical: (80 GB − 16 GB base) / 6.8 MB ≈ 9,412 adapters. Practical operational budget (§4, M10): ≥ 500 — an 18× headroom margin reserved for KV-cache and per-request activation memory shared across tenants, plus AdapterStore index/table overhead (chained from #2, §4).
  7. Wall-clock per epoch, Phase-0-scale Alpaca-cleaned fine-tune. 52,000 examples × ~350 avg tokens ≈ 18.2 M tokens; at M4's 3,500 tok/s floor: 18.2×10⁶ / 3,500 ≈ 5,200 s ≈ 87 min per epoch on a single H100 (chained from §4's M4 row).
  8. Hot-swap latency budget vs. request latency. M7's 15 ms swap is 15 / 500 = 3% of a typical 500 ms p99 generation-request budget — the swap cost is amortized, not a dominant tail-latency contributor (chained from §4's M7 row).
  9. FSDP 4→8 H100 scaling. At M11's ≥85% efficiency floor, 8-GPU wall-clock for a fixed workload is 4-GPU-time / (2 × 0.85) ≈ 4-GPU-time / 1.7 — not the naive /2 an ideal linear scaling would predict (chained from §4's M11 row).
