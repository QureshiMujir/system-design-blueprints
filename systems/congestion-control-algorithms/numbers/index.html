4. System Constraints

Defining property: this is a soft-real-time control loop clocked by ACK arrivals — the ACK→decision→pacing path must complete within a small fraction of the minimum inter-ACK gap, or the controller falls behind its own feedback and the pipe drains or overflows.

32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

Each number derives from the §4 System Constraints table (formula shown).

  1. BDP of a reference WAN path. BDP = BtlBw · RTprop. From §4 max 100 Gbps and RTT 100 µs–800 ms, take 10 Gbps × 40 ms: 10e9/8 · 0.040 = 50,000,000 B ≈ 50 MB in flight — cwnd must reach this to fill the pipe (MM2).
  2. Per-flow memory. §4: ≤ 512 B hot + ≤ 4 KB cold ≈ 4.5 KB/flow; round to 5 KB for headers.
  3. Flow-table RAM. flows · per-flow = 1,000,000 · 5 KB = 5 GB — matches the §4 ≤ 5 GB ceiling; sets the memory budget for a max-scale sim node.
  4. ACK CPU budget. §4 ACK p99 < 10 µs ⇒ one core sustains 1 / 10 µs = 100,000 ACKs/s at the tail; at p50 2 µs, 500,000 ACKs/s. So ~250 K active flows/core at ~1–2 ACKs/s each with headroom (the §29 ceiling).
  5. Pacing timer load. §4: 100 K armed timers, 1 ms wheel tick ⇒ 100,000 / 0.001 s = 100,000,000 timer-slot touches/s worst case — must be O(1)/slot (Varghese), else the wheel is the bottleneck (§29 #1).
  6. Simulator wall-clock for a run. §4: ≥ 2 M events/s/core. A 60 s, 1 Gbps, 1500 B-packet flow = 1e9/8/1500 · 60 ≈ 5,000,000 packets → ~2 events each = 10 M events → 10e6 / 2e6 = 5 s/core.
  7. Replay-log size. §4: ≤ 40 B/event. At 500 K events/s (§4 telemetry): 500,000 · 40 B · 3600 s = 72 GB/hour at full rate — so segment + rotate (ADR-11) and sample; the ≤ 512 MB/hour §4 target holds only at ≈ 3,600 events/s, i.e. logging is sampled ~140:1 at peak.
  8. Telemetry bandwidth. §4: 500 K events/s × ~64 B/metric-sample = 32 MB/s ⇒ the ≤ 1 GB buffer drains in 1 GB / 32 MB/s ≈ 31 s, matching the < 60 s pipeline-restart RTO (§4).
  9. FD budget check. §4: 250 K FDs/node; at 1 socket + 1 timerfd per flow ⇒ 125 K flows/node FD-limited — below the 250 K-active-flow CPU ceiling, so FDs bound before CPU on real sockets (sim flows use no FDs).
  10. Bufferbloat delay. A 1 Gbps link with a 1 MB dumb buffer adds queue delay = 1e6·8 / 1e9 = 8 ms — quantifies why loss-based CC (MM3) inflates RTT and why AQM/BBR target ≤ 1.5·BDP (M8).
