32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. CM1 — Audio bandwidth/connection. BW = codec_bitrate + header_bytes×8×pps. Opus 32 kbps + (12 RTP + 8 UDP + 20 IP = 40 B)×8×50 pps = 32 + 16 = 48 kbps/direction ⇒ 96 kbps bidirectional (chained from §4's Opus 6–64 kbps range).
  2. CM2 — Video bandwidth/connection. VP8 720p30 @ 1 Mbps target (§4), ~125 pps at ~1,000 B/packet ⇒ overhead = 125×40 B×8 = 40 kbps ⇒ ≈1.04 Mbps total.
  3. CM3 — Memory/connection. jitter buffer (300 ms×1.04 Mbps/8 ≈ 39 KB) + DTLS session state (~16 KB) + SCTP send/recv buffers (64 KB+64 KB) + Opus state (~50 KB)
    • VP8 decoder frame buffers (~2 MB @ 720p) ⇒ ≈2.2 MB/connection, well inside the ≤10 MB budget (§4) which reserves headroom for jitter spikes.
  4. CM4 — FD budget at 500 connections. FDs = connections × sockets_per_connection = 500×1 (muxed, §4) + active TURN allocations ⇒ ≈500–600 FDs for a full SFU process, well under typical ulimit -n (65536).
  5. CM5 — CPU/connection (SFU, forward-only). SRTP 2 µs×2 (enc+dec, M3) × (50 audio pps + 125 video pps) ≈ 2 µs×2×175 = 700 µs of CPU-second per wall-clock second per connection ⇒ negligible core-fraction, which is why M11's 500-connections/8-core target is achievable — the SFU forwards ciphertext, it never re-encodes (§1 scope).
  6. CM6 — TURN relay bandwidth overhead. overhead = connections × relayed_fraction × 2 × per_connection_bitrate (2× because relay doubles ingress+egress). At 1.04 Mbps (CM2) and 20% relayed of 500 connections: 500×0.2×2×1.04 Mbps ⇒ ≈208 Mbps additional TURN-server egress capacity required.
  7. CM7 — Fuzz corpus growth. libFuzzer typical throughput ~1,000 exec/s ⇒ 86.4M execs/24 h canary (M10); corpus is minimized (corpus_minimize.sh, §10) to stay inside the ≤500 MB storage budget (§4).
  8. CM8 — ICE gathering time. 2 STUN servers queried in parallel + 1 TURN allocate (1 RTT each) at 100 ms RTT ⇒ gathering ≈ 150–300 ms, leaving ≈2.7 s of the 3,000 ms M1 budget (§4) for connectivity-check pacing (Ta=50 ms × ~20 pairs ⇒ ≤1,000 ms worst-case serial, §16.7).
  9. CM9 — SCTP dead-peer detection ceiling. At RTO_max=60 s (§4), worst-case silent failure detection via SCTP alone takes up to 60 s; mitigated by ICE's STUN consent-freshness keepalive (every 15 s, §16.10) detecting failure roughly 4× faster.
