32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — Fleet memory ceiling. 250,000 connections × 128 KB steady-state ceiling (§4) = 32,000,000 KB = 32 GB/node, fitting a 64 GB node at 50% headroom for OS, telemetry, and slab fragmentation.
  • C2 — Worst-case per-connection metadata. 65,536 inflight packets × 256 B/packet ceiling (§4) = 16,777,216 B ≈ 16 MB worst case — 128× the 128 KB steady-state budget, meaning typical inflight occupancy must stay ≤ 0.8% of the ceiling under normal operation.
  • C3 — Replay-log append rate. 1,000,000 events/sec (§4) × ~64 B/event (postcard-framed ReplayEvent, §11) = 64,000,000 B/sec = 64 MB/sec sustained; × 1.35 storage amplification (§4) = 86.4 MB/sec worst case.
  • C4 — Replay-log daily volume. 64 MB/sec (C3) × 86,400 sec/day = 5,529,600 MB/day ≈ 5.4 TB/day/node at sustained peak, driving the retention/rotation policy in §27.
  • C5 — Timer-wheel slot baseline. 250,000 connections × 3 packet-number spaces × 1 loss timer each (I8) = 750,000 concurrently addressable timer slots, the ADR-23 wheel's O(1)-insert design point.
  • C6 — FD budget exactness. 32 workers/node (§4) × 4 sockets/worker (§4) = 128 FDs/node = the §4 FD budget exactly, zero headroom — any additional per-worker socket forces a §4 budget revision.
  • C7 — Correlated PTO-storm spike. 250,000 connections × 6 worst-case consecutive PTO backoffs before persistent congestion (I9) = 1,500,000 timer re-arms in a worst-case correlated-failure window = 2× the C5 baseline the wheel must transiently absorb.
  • C8 — Pathological ACK-processing CPU bound. 250,000 connections × 1 ACK per §4's 1 ms RTT floor = 250,000,000 ACKs/sec fleet-wide × 5 µs/ACK p50 (§4) = 1,250 core-seconds/sec of work — real traffic is not synchronized at the RTT floor across all connections, so this bounds only the pathological worst case, not expected load.
  • C9 — Per-shard connection density. 250,000 connections ÷ 32 workers (§4) = 7,813 connections/worker, each needing its own timer-wheel slot (C5) and slab region — sizes the per-worker memory arena at 7,813 × 128 KB ceiling = 1,000,064 KB ≈ 977 MB/worker.
  • C10 — Availability budget in absolute time. 99.99% (§4) means ≤ 0.01% × 525,600 min/year = 52.56 minutes/year of unplanned recovery-subsystem downtime — roughly 13.14 minutes per quarter (52.56 ÷ 4) if evenly distributed, the number FM-1 through FM-12's mean-time-to-detect must collectively stay under.
  • C11 — Conformance-suite CI budget. ~40 fixtures (§21.4) × an assumed 2 s/fixture parallel-execution target = 80 fixture-seconds of work, divided across ≥ 8 parallel CI runners (§21.7) = 10 s wall-clock, keeping the conformance gate from becoming the release-velocity bottleneck.
  • C12 — Soak-test event volume. 72 hours (§21.4) × 3,600 sec/hour × up to 1,000,000 events/sec (§4) = 259,200,000,000 events theoretical ceiling per soaked node, motivating the aggregated (not per-event-retained) soak-telemetry policy in §21.7.
