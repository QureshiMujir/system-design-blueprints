Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

Numbers an expert computes before designing, each derived from §4.1. 8-bit 4:2:0 assumed (1.5 bytes/pixel).

  • C1 — Raw frame size (1080p). 1920 × 1080 × 1.5 = 3.11 MB/frame; the ≤ 6 MB/ref DPB budget (§4.1) leaves headroom for 10-bit samples or padded search borders.
  • C2 — DPB memory (4 refs, 1080p). 4 × 3.11 MB = 12.4 MB of reference frames resident; within the ≤ 128 MB working-memory budget (§4.1) alongside transform scratch and entropy tables.
  • C3 — Motion-vector storage (1080p, 4 refs). (1920/4)×(1080/4) × 4 refs × 4 B = 129 600 × 4 × 4 = 2.07 MB/frame (§4.1), pre-allocated per frame, not heap-per-MB.
  • C4 — Macroblocks per frame. 1080p = (1920/16)×(⌈1080/16⌉) = 120 × 68 = 8 160 MB; 720p = 80 × 45 = 3 600 MB — the unit over which all per-MB budgets (§4.1) accumulate.
  • C5 — Per-MB time budget (720p encode). ≥ 5 fps ⇒ 200 ms/frame ÷ 3 600 MB = ≈ 55 µs/MB average, well under the 5 ms p99 worst case (§4.1) — i.e. the worst MB may be ~90× the average.
  • C6 — Output bitrate (720p, QP 28). Raw 1280×720×1.5 = 1.38 MB/frame × 5 fps = 6.9 MB/s; at the ~20:1 ratio (§4.1) ⇒ ≈ 2.8 Mbps bitstream.
  • C7 — Decode/encode asymmetry. Decode target 30 fps vs encode 5 fps at 720p (§4.1) = 6× faster, because decode skips motion search (MM3) — it only copies via supplied vectors.
  • C8 — Hot-loop working set. One MB = 256 B luma + 2×64 B chroma = 384 B; a ±16 search window ≈ (16+32)² ≈ 2 304 luma samples/ref — the whole hot loop fits the ≤ 256 KB L2 budget (§4.1).
  • C9 — Transform operations per frame. Per MB: 16 luma + 8 chroma = 24 4×4 transforms; 1080p = 8 160 × 24 ≈ 196 K transforms/frame, the dominant integer-arithmetic load.
  • C10 — Bitstream buffer ceiling. ≤ 2× uncompressed (§4.1) bounds NAL byte-stuffing worst case: 1080p ⇒ ≤ 6.2 MB output buffer even on incompressible (QP 0) input.
  • C11 — Drift budget. 0 bits over a 300-frame GOP (§4.1): any nonzero reconstruction difference at frame N propagates to all N+1…300, so the tolerance is exactly zero (MM1).
  • C12 — Conformance corpus footprint. ≈ 500 MB JVT set (see Scalability Analysis) stored via LFS; fetched only on the conformance CI step, not every build.
