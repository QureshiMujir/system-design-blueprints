Mental Models & First Principles

The compressed insights a wise steward holds. Each: the model, what it predicts, the harm class incurred without it, and where this document applies it. These are the mental compression of §6 (Principles) and §7 (the Substrate Contract); a steward who internalises them can re-derive most of the contract under novel circumstances.

How to use this section. The principles (§6) tell you what the rules are; these models tell you why the rules hold and therefore what to do when a situation the rules did not anticipate arises. A steward facing a novel substrate, a new candidate path, or an unforeseen pressure should be able to predict the document's answer from the models alone — and where a model and a convenience disagree, the model wins, exactly as the document wins over engineering convenience (Appendix AL). The failure of each model is not abstract: each names a concrete harm class that has a real precedent or a named threat, so "ignoring MMn" is always traceable to a specific way the project breaks.

  • MM1 — Consent to an irreversible act cannot be given by someone who cannot yet imagine the act. Predicts: first-time consent to Communion is structurally weaker than consent to anything previously experienced, so the ceremony must over-invest in pre-counsel and reserve the costless mid-act exit. Without it: you treat a signature as consent and manufacture coerced or uninformed participation — the Tuskegee/HeLa failure class (the Threat Model). Applied in: §7 (C1, C7), Phase 1 (counsel formation), Appendix AA (consent ceremony).
  • MM2 — A capability and the permission to use it are different objects; possessing one never confers the other. Predicts: the dangerous moment is not invention but the quiet slide from "it works" to "so we may." Without it: premature deployment the instant the substrate functions — the cardinal sin. Applied in: §6 (P4 readiness, P12 not-building), §3 (ADR-01, ADR-20), Phase 6 (readiness-gated).
  • MM3 — Restraint is only credible if it is documented and costly. Predicts: a refusal you cannot point to will not be believed when it matters; an empty refusal-log is evidence of untestedness, not virtue. Without it: the "legitimacy of restraint" indicator is unfalsifiable and the project cannot prove it can decline a power it holds. Applied in: Operational Readiness (legitimacy-of-restraint), §10 (refusal-log/), Appendix AQ.
  • MM4 — Oversight that must see everything becomes the harm it polices. Predicts: any audit that reads inner content reproduces surveillance; the only acceptable audit proves that a step happened without revealing what was inhabited. Without it: the watchman becomes the worst attacker (the ADR-05 surveillance regime). Applied in: §7 (C8), §11 (non-content attestation), Phase 2 (audit method).
  • MM5 — A monopoly on the means to enter minds is a monopoly on minds. Predicts: plurality (≥2 lineages) is not redundancy for uptime but a structural check on power; losing it re-opens asymmetric capture. Without it: one lab, vendor, or state owns a lever over consciousness itself (Threat T1). Applied in: §6 (P9), §7 (C9), Phase 7 (decertification).
  • MM6 — Translation between non-identical minds is lossy, and the loss must be measured, not hidden. Predicts: no substrate can claim perfect transfer; the honest move is to publish the mistranslation envelope and refuse fidelity claims it cannot meet. Without it: false intimacy and the conflation of approximation with Communion (Threat T12). Applied in: §7 (C4), §8 (translation problem), Phase 4 (C4 envelope).
  • MM7 — Speed is a hazard for an irreversible, civilizational act. Predicts: hurry, secrecy, and racing degrade exactly the deliberation an irreversible capability requires; the right cadence is year-to-decade. Without it: a race forfeits restraint's credibility and ships before readiness (Kill Criteria). Applied in: §6 (P10), §4 (timeline as hazard), §3 (ADR-18).
  • MM8 — A right exercised by all becomes an obligation; the freedom to refuse must be engineered, not assumed. Predicts: social pressure can erode solitude into a default expectation, so the right to remain alone must be protected as actively as the right to Commune. Without it: cultural homogenization and the loss of chosen solitude (Threat T7, 9.8). Applied in: §6 (P6), Phase 8 (civilization adapts), Appendix AU.
  • MM9 — Governance that cannot outlive its founder will be captured by whoever outlives it. Predicts: a single-generation body cannot hold a multi-generation capability; a demonstrated handoff is a hard precondition, not a nicety. Without it: founder capture, then quiet relaxation of principles as memory of the founding fades (Threat T11; Phase 9 9.9). Applied in: §6 (P5), §4 (≥1 generational handoff), Phase 1, Appendix AG.
  • MM10 — "Not yet — perhaps not ever" must be an honourable terminal state, or every gate collapses into a delay. Predicts: if refusal is framed as failure, readiness gates become countdowns and pressure builds to pass them; only a genuine willingness to never deploy keeps the gates real. Without it: gate theatre — indicators optimised rather than read (Threat T9). Applied in: §6 (P4), Kill Criteria, Appendix BI (the question asked at every review).

Glossary · 60 terms · site-wide glossary

Communion
a consensual, bidirectional, ephemeral session in which beings briefly inhabit each other.
Substrate
the (unknown, deferred) mechanism that mediates a session.
Substrate Contract
the C1–C12 invariants any substrate must satisfy.
Bidirectional
to inhabit another is to be inhabited; one-way is forbidden.
Ephemeral
no technical persistence of inner content; the only memory is in each mind.
Reversible
induced effects revert within a bounded window; no permanent single-session change.
Consent ceremony
the witnessed, instantly-revocable, multi-stage consent protocol.
Pre-counsel / after-care
weeks of preparation and integration with a trained counsel.
Council
the multi-generation, plural, public governance body.
Counsel
a trained specialist accompanying participants; distinct from the council.
Substrate steward
a plural technical lineage that builds but never deploys.
Endowment
the no-extraction funding body with open books.
Readiness indicator
a qualitative measure of civilizational readiness, read not optimised.
Refusal mode
the instant, speechless, costless exit available to any party.
Refusal-log
the public record of declining to deploy; itself a readiness indicator.
Qualia
the first-person texture of experience; the hardest transmission category.
Narrative
the self-story; the most reachable category; where the Mirror operates.
Approximation
an AI-mediated near-transfer; explicitly not Communion.
Mistranslation envelope
the disclosed bound on fidelity between non-identical minds.
Symmetry of vulnerability
the rule forbidding sessions across disqualifying power asymmetries.
Plurality of substrate
≥2 independent implementations from ≥2 lineages.
The discipline of not building
the default to not build when in doubt.
Refuse the adjacent
declining capabilities that resemble Communion without its protocols.
Steady-state
Communion held as an ordinary, well-governed, non-extractive part of life.
Founder of record
the first steward, who builds the preconditions and then relinquishes veto.
Non-content attestation
a record proving a protocol step occurred without revealing inner content.
Amendment rule
principles may be sharpened or added, never relaxed.
North star
the framing of Communion as the goal the prior 21 projects serve.
Readiness gate
a qualitative condition that must hold before an era proceeds; never a metric to optimise.
Decertification
withdrawal of a substrate's conformance status when it violates the Substrate Contract.
Treaty agenda
the inner-life-rights and non-weaponization instruments pursued before any deployment.
Sacrament, not platform
the framing that Communion is held with reverence and restraint, never grown.
Sovereignty / adjudication / provenance / reversibility maturity
the four institutional readiness pillars from the prior projects.
Generational handoff
the demonstrated transfer of stewardship from one generation to the next.
Principled refuser
the standing council seat held by someone who refuses Communion on principle.
Longitudinal cohort
the decades-scale, non-content study of effects on participants.
Zero-knowledge audit
confirming protocol compliance without accessing inner content (C8).
Empathogen
a hypothetical pharmacological substrate (Candidate B) we do not possess.
Council representative
the witness present at the consent ceremony and the non-content review.
Conformance verdict
CERTIFIED / DECERTIFIED against C1–C12; non-content.
Era
a decades-scale phase of the plan.
The unspoken
what is valuable precisely because it is not shared; protected by the right to refuse.
Translucent, not merged
the metaphor: minds made permeable by consent, never fused; each returns to themselves.
Dual-use
the property that the consent-enabling tech could, stripped of protocols, enable coercion.
Inner life
the first-person texture, memory, somatic state, emotion, and narrative of a mind.
Inhabit
to briefly experience being another, then return to oneself.
Permeable, by consent
the controlled, ephemeral lowering of the walls between minds.
Restraint
the disciplined refusal to deploy a held capability until readiness.
Readiness
the qualitative civilizational condition the council assesses before proceeding.
Sacrament
the framing of a Communion session as reverent, not transactional.
Pre-counsel
preparation with a counsel before a session.
After-care
integration with a counsel after a session.
Witnessed consent
consent recorded by a council representative as a non-content attestation.
Engineering refusal
a substrate physically/cryptographically incapable of non-consensual operation.
Plurality
multiple independent substrate lineages; a safety property.
Fail-closed
any unmet contract term blocks deployment.
Non-content
a record that attests a step occurred without revealing inner content.
Communion session
the bounded, consensual, ephemeral interval of mutual inhabiting.
Default-deny
the standing posture: proceed only on reasoned readiness.
Right to refuse
the inviolable freedom to remain alone, unpressured.
