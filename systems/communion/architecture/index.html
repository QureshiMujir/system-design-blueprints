3. Architectural Overview — Candidate Substrates

The honest engineering statement is: the architecture is unknown. What is fixed is the set of commitments any substrate must inherit. The diagram shows the protocol/institutional architecture (buildable) wrapping an unknown substrate (deferred).

                         +---------------------------------------------+
                         |        MULTI-GENERATION COUNCIL             |
                         |  (governs; public, legible, slow; plural)   |
                         +-----------------------+---------------------+
                                                 | authorises / audits (no inner content)
                                                 v
   pre-counsel --> CONSENT CEREMONY --> [  SUBSTRATE (UNKNOWN)  ] --> AFTER-CARE --> council review
   (weeks)        (witnessed, revocable)  | bidirectional, ephemeral,  (weeks)        (non-content)
        ^                                 | consent-bound, reversible |
        |                                 | plural impls, refusal mode|
        |                                 +---------------------------+
        |                                       ^        ^        ^
        |                          Candidate A (Neural/BMI)  |   Candidate C (AI-mediated = deep Mirror)
        |                          Candidate B (Biotech)  Candidate D (Hybrid)  Candidate E (not yet imagined)
        |
   COUNSELS (trained specialists)            SUBSTRATE STEWARDS (plural lineages; build, never deploy)
                                             ENDOWMENT (no extraction; open books)

Multi-generation council. The governing body that holds Communion; public, legible, slow, plural across ≥3 generations and many disciplines (incl. a member who refuses Communion in principle). Authorises and audits sessions without accessing inner content.

Consent ceremony. A formal, witnessed, instantly-revocable exchange of consent in the council representative's presence; the gate before any session.

Substrate (unknown). The mechanism itself — deferred. Candidates: A Neural/BMI (high-bandwidth bidirectional coupling + translation; leading late-21st-century candidate; highest physical risk); B Biotech (engineered neuromodulation or pharmacological empathogen; speculative; long-tail side effects); C AI-mediated (a much deeper Mirror constructing a transient first-person model — closest to reachable this century, and most easily mistaken for the real thing; treated as approximation, not Communion); D Hybrid (the likely outcome — BMI + AI translation + biotech safety windows + the prior projects' crypto); E Not-yet-imagined (the option this document refuses to foreclose).

Architectural commitments (all substrates). Strict bidirectionality; hard ephemerality; cryptographic consent; reversibility of effects; substrate diversity (≥2 independent implementations); instantaneous non-shameable refusal modes.

Counsels. Trained specialists (contemplative, somatic, ethical) who accompany participants before, during, and after; a distinct body from the council, bound by the strictest secrecy.

Substrate stewards. Plural technical lineages (universities, foundations, public consortia) that maintain substrate implementations; they report to the council and do not deploy.

Endowment. Funds the work under a charter that forbids extraction; trustees public; books open; dissolution routes funds to council-named causes, never private actors.

8. Domain Model — What Communion Transmits

Communion is a transmission of inner life between minds. The categories below are annotated with this-century tractability.

Category What Tractability
Qualia the first-person texture (the redness of red, the heaviness of sorrow) deeply open — the hard problem; without it, it is not Communion
Memory episodic + semantic (a childhood kitchen; the day a father died) partial — active decoding research; raises secret-sharing consent issues
Somatic state the felt body (posture, breath, hunger, pain, the felt sense before thought) moderate — interoception/somatic-marker research; needs sensing + induction
Emotion affect as lived moderate→good for gross categories; deeply open for fine inner texture; needs narrative to disambiguate
Narrative the story by which a person makes sense of themselves most reachable — modern AI approximates much of it; The Mirror operates here

Not transmitted. Skills (inhabiting a violinist does not make you one); specific knowledge (facts are not bused; the felt sense of knowing is); identity (no merger; each returns to themselves); persistent connection (it ends; no ongoing channel).

The translation problem. Two nervous systems are not identical; what is a quale in one need not be in the other. Some translation is inevitable; the Substrate Contract (C4) requires it be modelled, disclosed, and bounded. The discipline is to never claim fidelity that does not exist. Narrative-only transmission is approximation, not Communion (§12, Candidate C).

8.1 Session lifecycle (state machine)

  • PRE_COUNSEL (days–weeks) → preparation with a trained counsel (contemplative, somatic, ethical).
  • CONSENT_CEREMONY → formal, witnessed, instantly-revocable exchange before a council representative.
  • SESSION → the substrate-mediated interval (minutes–tens of minutes); refusal mode always live.
  • AFTER_CARE (days–weeks) → integration with the same counsel; the participants have been changed.
  • COUNCIL_REVIEW → confidential, non-content attestation that the protocol was honoured.
  • Any state → REFUSED/ENDED → instant, costless exit; nothing inner persists.

8.2 Worked arcs (illustrative; no such substrate exists)

  • The dying and the loved one. A terminally ill person and their child Commune once, under counsel, with weeks of preparation and after-care; the child carries forward an indelible memory of having been their parent. Beachhead, if it ever happens at all.
  • Refusal mid-session. A participant invokes the speechless, costless refusal mode; the session ends for all parties; no record, no judgment (C7).
  • Disqualifying asymmetry. An employer and employee attempt to consent; the substrate's symmetry screen refuses to run the session regardless of stated consent (C6/Principle 8).

8.3 Failure / refusal paths

  • Consent unfree or coerced ⇒ ceremony fails; no session (C1).
  • Substrate capable of recording inner content ⇒ decertified (C2).
  • Substrate distorts/persuades within the session ⇒ does not deploy (C3).
  • Only one implementation exists ⇒ no deployment (C9).
  • A non-council actor reaches a working substrate first ⇒ civilizational emergency (Kill Criteria).

Design principles (§6)

  • Consent, always. No session without fully informed, uncoerced, revocable, contemporaneous consent of every party — a multi-stage protocol, not a checkbox. Communion without consent is not Communion; it is assault.
  • Reversibility — every session ephemeral. Each session ends; nothing technical persists of its inner content. No recordings, no replay, no third-party access. The only persistence is the memory each carries within their own mind.
  • No extraction. Communion is a public good or a sacrament, never a business. No party may extract revenue, attention, data, persuasion, or political advantage from it.
  • Readiness — do not deploy until civilization is morally ready. A capability that exists is not therefore to be used; restraint is a first-order output. If the readiness criteria are unmet, the answer is "not yet — perhaps not ever in this generation."
  • Elder-counsel — no deployment without a multi-generation governance body. Where no such council exists, the project does not deploy; it builds the council.
  • Solitude as a positive value. Communion makes solitude chosen; the right to remain alone inside one's skull is sacred and may never be diminished by social pressure to Commune.
  • Bidirectional only. To inhabit another is to be inhabited. Reading without being read is a different, forbidden technology.
  • Symmetry of vulnerability. Forbidden between parties whose power asymmetry makes consent unfree; the council enumerates and updates the disqualifying classes.
  • Plurality of substrate. No single substrate, vendor, lab, or jurisdiction may monopolise the means; diversity is itself a safety property.
  • Public, legible, slow. All council decisions are public, legible to the lay reader, and made on year-scale horizons. Speed, secrecy, and hurry are suspicious.
  • Refuse the adjacent. Refuse capabilities that resemble Communion without its protocols — engagement-maximising "deep empathy" media, persuasion-tuned affective AI, surveillance-fed mind-reading, weaponised affect.
  • The discipline of not building. When in doubt, do not build. When the choice is between a maybe-safe thing and not building it, the default is do not build. The 21 prior projects exist to make this default possible without sacrificing the goal.
