32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. Join-order search space (naïve). Distinct bushy join trees for N labelled relations = Catalan(N-1) × N!. At N = 10: Catalan(9) = 4,862, 10! = 3,628,800 ⇒ ≈ 1.76 × 10^10 — the textbook "10-relation joins have billions of plans" figure (Selinger-era motivation, §5).
  2. DPccp worst-case memo size. Bounded by 3^N connected-subgraph/complement-pair enumerations (Moerkotte & Neumann 2006, §5). At N = 16 (§4 cap): 3^16 ≈ 4.30 × 10^7 — the §4 plan-space-size row, derived directly.
  3. DPccp practical memo size (sparse TPC-H-shaped graphs). Empirically low thousands of memo entries — orders of magnitude below the worst-case bound in (2), because real join graphs are sparse (each relation joins to 1–3 others), not fully connected.
  4. Memo memory footprint at N = 16. ≈ 2,000 practical groups (from (3)) × ~5 fields/ GroupExpression × 8 bytes/field ≈ 80 KB core structure, well inside the M7/§4 64 MB ceiling — the ceiling exists for pathological (near-worst-case) graphs, not the typical case.
  5. Planning-time budget per relation-set evaluation. plan_timeout_ms (5,000 ms, §4) ÷ worst case 3^16 probes ≈ 1.16 × 10^-4 ms/probe budget if every probe were reached — in practice the sparse-graph case (3) leaves orders of magnitude of slack, which is exactly why the p99 target (§4: 200 ms) sits far under the hard timeout.
  6. Cardinality-error compounding across a join chain. For per-join relative error e = 0.1 (I8 domain) over k = 5 joins, compounded multiplicatively: 1 - (1-e)^k = 1 - 0.9^5 ≈ 0.41 — a 5-way join chain can carry ~41% compounded relative error even with a "good" 10%-per-join estimator, motivating the M3 target (within 10× actual, not 10%).
  7. HyperLogLog sketch size. 2^14 registers (ADR-10) × 6 bits/register (dense HLL++ encoding) / 8 bits/byte = 12,288 bytes ≈ 12 KB/column sketch; with alignment/padding overhead this rounds to the ≈ 16 KB/sketch figure used for capacity planning at thousands of columns.
  8. Statistics-build throughput requirement. M8 requires 1 GB TPC-H processed in < 60 s ⇒ ≥ 1,024 MB / 60 s ≈ 17.1 MB/s sustained scan-plus-histogram-build throughput — the floor qp stats build's reservoir-sampling implementation must clear.
  9. RelationId/bitset capacity ceiling. RelationId: u8 (§11.1) addresses up to 256 distinct IDs, but the DPccp fast path packs a RelationSet into a single u64 bitset ⇒ a hard 64-relation cap on the exact-search representation (§4), independent of the u8 ID width itself.
  10. EXPLAIN size budget. M6 targets < 1 KB/query at ~1 line/operator; at ≈ 40 bytes/line, that budget covers ≈ 25 operators comfortably — a 22-way TPC-H join (≈ 40–50 operator-tree nodes including enforcers) sits close to the budget, which is why §21.6/§21.8 name an explicit summary-mode fallback rather than letting the budget silently blow past 1 KB.
  11. Statistics staleness window in row terms. At the I11/§4 20%-drift trigger, a table with a stats-build row_count_estimate of 10,000,000 tolerates drift up to 2,000,000 rows (insert/ delete/update net) before ANALYZE/replan is triggered — the number an operator sizing an ANALYZE cadence schedule actually needs.
  12. Adaptive replan divergence trigger, in cost terms. Default adaptive_replan_threshold = 5× (§21.2); combined with the M3 accuracy target (within 10× on ≥ 80% of joins), roughly 1-in-5 of the "acceptable" estimates (those between 5× and 10× off) are exactly the ones the Phase-1+ adaptive hook (§21) is designed to still catch and correct at runtime.
