Core schema & types (§11)

ScheduleRecord (struct)
  schedule_id: UuidV7
  tenant_id: TenantId
  trigger: TriggerDefinition
  next_fire_at: Timestamp
  retry_policy: RetryPolicy
  version: u64
  metadata: BoundedMap<String, String>
TriggerDefinition (enum)
  Delayed(fire_in_ms: u64)
  Cron(expression: CronExpr, timezone: TzId)
  FixedInterval(period_ms: u64, anchor: Timestamp)
  OneShot(at: Timestamp)
WheelBucket (struct)
  bucket_id: u64
  expiration_tick: u64
  timers: IntrusiveLinkedList<TimerRef>
LeaseToken (struct)
  shard_id: u32
  epoch: u64
  owner_node: NodeId
  expires_at: MonotonicTime
DispatchEnvelope (struct)
  dispatch_id: Uuid
  schedule_id: Uuid
  attempt: u32
  deadline: Timestamp
  payload_ref: BlobPointer
RetryPolicy (struct)
  max_attempts: u32
  backoff_strategy: BackoffStrategy
  max_backoff_ms: u64
  jitter_mode: JitterMode
BackoffStrategy (enum) Fixed | Exponential | ExponentialDecorrelated
JitterMode (enum) None | Full | Decorrelated
ShardAssignment (struct)
  shard_id: u32
  lease: LeaseToken
  key_range: HashRange
ReplayCursor (struct)
  segment_id: u64
  byte_offset: u64
  last_applied_lsn: u64
TenantQuota (struct)
  tenant_id: TenantId
  max_active_timers: u64
  max_insert_rate_per_sec: u32
  window: QuotaWindow
TimerState (enum)
  Created | Durable | Scheduled | Dispatched | Acked
  RetryPending | DeadLetter | Cancelled
AuditEvent (struct)
  event_id: Uuid
  tenant_id: TenantId
  actor: ActorId
  action: AuditAction
  at: Timestamp
