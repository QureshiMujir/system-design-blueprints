Defining property: the forward path is a hard-real-time-adjacent budget (single-digit milliseconds, P3) while the rewind path is a correctness-adjacent budget (tens of seconds, willing to trade latency for validated safety) — every design decision in this blueprint resolves ties in favor of that asymmetry.

32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. Hot-tier storage growth/day = throughput × record size × amplification × seconds/day = 50,000 ops/s × 400 B × 1.6 (§4) × 86,400 s ≈ 2.76 TB/day/instance before compaction.
  2. Cold-tier monthly volume = 2.76 TB/day × 30-day hot retention ≈ 82.8 TB/month migrated to S3 before compression (chained from #1).
  3. Idempotency LRU cache capacity = 512 MiB budget (§4) ÷ ≈150 B per key+metadata ≈ 3.4M resident keys — derived directly from the §4 memory ceiling.
  4. Max concurrent proxied app connections = 65,536 hard FD ulimit (§4) ÷ 2 FDs per proxied connection ≈ 32,768 connections/instance.
  5. Sandbox spin-up budget breakdown — at an assumed 500 MB/s managed-PITR restore throughput, a 100 GB DB restores in 100,000 MB ÷ 500 MB/s = 200 s ≈ 3.3 min, leaving ≈ 1.7 min of the M6 < 5 min target for forward-replay.
  6. Agent-quota overhead budget = 100 actions/hour/agent (§21.2) × 50ms p99 wrap overhead (M7) = 5s/hour of serialized overhead per agent — negligible against the hour-long quota window.
  7. Concurrent-rewind FD headroom = 100 concurrent rewinds cluster-wide (§4) × ≈50 FDs/active rewind (per-service fan-out) = 5,000 FDs, a ≈13× headroom against the 65,536 hard ulimit.
  8. Throughput headroom before manual sharding = ≈200,000 ops/sec single-instance ceiling (ADR-03) ÷ 50,000 ops/sec baseline (§4) = 4× headroom before a tenant must be sharded across instances.
  9. Ingress bandwidth at baseline = 50,000 ops/s × 400 B/op = 20 MB/s sustained gRPC ingress/instance — well under a 1 Gbps (125 MB/s) NIC, leaving headroom for the pre-image SELECT's doubled round trips (§13.7).
  10. Phase-0 proof-of-correctness storage floor = 10,000-record synthetic corpus (§12.5) × 400 B ≈ 4 MB — confirms Phase 0's scope is correctness, not scale.
