32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. Image bandwidth/session/hour = 60 scenes/hr (avg 60 s/scene, §8.4) × 300 KB/image ≈ 18 MB/hour/session (pre-cache).
  2. Region image egress at max concurrency = 18 MB × 5,000 sessions (§4 max concurrency) = 90,000 MB/hr; at ≥35% cache-hit rate (§4), origin-served fraction = 65% → ≈ 58.5 GB/hr origin egress/region.
  3. Aggregate Composer-context RAM/region = 256 MB ceiling/session (§4) × 5,000 concurrent sessions = 1.28 TB upper-bound resident memory before hierarchical-memory summarization (deferred) reduces it.
  4. Minimum Edge/API instances/region for FD headroom = 8,192 FDs/instance (§4) ÷ ~5 FDs/session (1 WebSocket + ~4 adapter connections) ≈ 1,638 sessions/instance; 5,000 sessions ÷ 1,638 ≈ 4 instances minimum (FD-bound, before CPU/latency sizing).
  5. Adapter calls/hour/region = 3 adapters/scene (text, image, music, Phase 1) × 60 scenes/hr × 5,000 sessions = 900,000 calls/hour/region.
  6. Region-wide cost ceiling exposure = $0.40/SH ceiling (§4, Phase 1) × 5,000 concurrent sessions = $2,000/hr theoretical peak, i.e. ≈ $48,000/day — the number I13's rolling-window gate exists to keep in check.
  7. PWS Scene-metadata storage/world/year = 1,095 scenes/year (3/day × 365) × ~2 KB/Scene row × 1.4 amplification (§4) ≈ 3.1 MB/year/world; at 5,000 WAD retained a full year ≈ 15.5 GB/year region-wide (excludes asset blobs, held separately in object storage).
  8. Cache-driven cost reduction = resume-session fraction (~60%, tracking the ≥40% persistent-world-recall target) × cache-hit rate (≥35%, §4) ≈ 21% of all scene-asset generations avoided, a direct ¢/SH reduction versus a no-cache baseline — the number the Phase 8 cache-hit target is chasing.
  9. Global concurrent-session ceiling (3 regions) = 5,000/region (§4) × 3 regions = 15,000 global concurrent sessions before a 4th region is required.
  10. Cancellation-path throughput requirement = 900,000 calls/hr (item 5) × ~5% viewer skip-ahead rate ≈ 45,000 cancellations/hour/region, each requiring ≥95% cost-reclaim within 2 s (I11) — sizes the cancellation subsystem independent of the render subsystem.
  11. Safety Classifier Ensemble call volume = 900,000 adapter calls/hr (item 5) × 1 classification per output ≈ 900,000 classifications/hour/region, each with an added-latency budget of ≤300 ms p95 (M9, §28) — sizes the classifier replica count needed to stay off the scene-latency critical path.
  12. Phase-9 video cost crossover point = at ¢/SH ceiling $2.50 (§4) and self-hosted GPU-hour cost of roughly $2–4/GPU-hour (2026-era spot pricing), a self-hosted video clip becomes cost-competitive with a frontier-API clip once per-session GPU utilization exceeds ≈60% (i.e., the GPU is busy more than 36 minutes of every stream-hour) — the threshold ADR-14's "when self-hosted wins" column is keyed to.
