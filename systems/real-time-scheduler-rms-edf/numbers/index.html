32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — Task metadata memory. TaskSpec (96 B) + TaskRuntimeState (80 B) = 176 B/task × 4096 tasks = 720,896 B ≈ 0.69 MB, inside the §4 256 MB ceiling.
  • C2 — Run-queue overhead. BinaryHeap<TaskId> entry ≈ 24 B × 512 tasks/core = 12,288 B ≈ 12 KB/core, inside the §4 256 KB per-core cache budget; × 32 cores = 393,216 B ≈ 384 KB system-wide.
  • C3 — Trace buffer sustained rate. TraceEvent 40 B × 200,000 events/s × 1 s window = 8,000,000 B ≈ 7.63 MB/s raw; × the §4 3× JSON amplification = 22.9 MB/s sustained chrome-trace export rate.
  • C4 — Wakeup latency budget. timerfd expiry read 2 µs + scheduler lock acquire 3 µs + select_next 4 µs + wake syscall 6 µs = 15 µs = the §4 wakeup p50 budget exactly.
  • C5 — Dispatch latency budget. run-queue pop 2 µs + affinity-mask check 1 µs + preemption-signal delivery 5 µs = 8 µs = the §4 dispatch p50 budget exactly.
  • C6 — Admission cost at 512 tasks. 512 tasks × 512 interference iterations × 55 ns/iteration = 14,417,920 ns ≈ 14.4 ms, inside the §4 15 ms admission budget.
  • C7 — Full-deployment memory. C1 (0.69 MB) + C2 (0.384 MB) + a fixed 64 MB trace-ring allocation = 65.1 MB, inside the §4 256 MB ceiling with ≈4× headroom for fragmentation and allocator overhead.
  • C8 — FD usage. 32 timerfd handles (1/core) + 1 trace-export socket + ≤16 log/metric files = 49 FDs, inside the §4 4096 FD budget by ≈84×.
  • C9 — Availability realized. 99.9%/24 h (§4) = 86.4 s allowed downtime/day; the §4 30 s RTO permits 2 full restart cycles inside that budget before breaching the SLA.
  • C10 — NUMA penalty amortization. 120 ns/cross-node access (§4, budgeted) × 8 worst-case cross-node accesses per dispatch decision = 960 ns ≈ 6% of the §4 15 µs wakeup budget.
  • C11 — Jitter budget vs. shortest period. 100 µs p99 jitter (§4) ÷ 5 ms shortest configured period = 2% relative-jitter ceiling for the reference sensor-class workload (§8.6).
  • C12 — Startup budget. crate init 50 ms + tracing subscriber bring-up 80 ms + timerfd arm at 2 ms/core × 32 cores = 64 ms + admission preflight 15 ms (C6) = 209 ms, inside the §4 500 ms startup ceiling with ≈58% headroom.
