32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — Experiment Memory Ceiling. 512 MB per experiment × 32 concurrent experiments = 16.0 GB, matching the 16 GB reference lab-host RAM budget from §4.
  • C2 — Telemetry Queue Bytes. 100,000 events × 32 B/ProbeSample (§11, padded) = 3.2 MB per queue, negligible against the 512 MB per-experiment ceiling.
  • C3 — Frame Fixed Overhead. 8 B frame_id + 16 B experiment_id + 32 B checksum + 8 B footer = 64 B fixed header/footer per ReplayFrame, amortized over thousands of samples to stay inside the ≤ 1.4x storage-amplification budget (§4).
  • C4 — Max Artifact Sample Capacity. 5,368,709,120 B artifact budget ÷ 32 B/ProbeSample = 167,772,160 samples per artifact, exceeding any single experiment's realistic 100k-event queue by 1,677x.
  • C5 — Trace Ingestion Rate at Queue-Full. 100,000 events ÷ 20 ms p50 ingestion-latency budget (§4) = 5,000,000 events/s sustained enqueue rate required at a queue-full transient, matching M7's target exactly.
  • C6 — FD Budget Aggregate. 64 FDs/experiment × 32 concurrent experiments = 2,048 FDs, under a typical 1,048,576 system-wide FD ceiling but requiring the per-process soft ulimit raised from the 1,024 default.
  • C7 — Storage Amplification Absolute. 5 GB max artifact × 1.4x amplification factor (§4) = 7.0 GB worst-case on-disk footprint per max-size experiment.
  • C8 — Skew vs. Probe Resolution. 5 us sync-skew budget ÷ 500 ns p50 probe latency (§4) = 10x, meaning skew dominates raw probe signal unless actively compensated (§15 jitter compensation).
  • C9 — Typical Concurrent RAM vs. Host. 32 experiments × 128 MB typical (not max) = 4.096 GB typical steady-state, 8x headroom under the C1 16 GB worst-case ceiling.
  • C10 — RTO vs. Read Throughput. 5 GB max artifact ÷ 10 s RTO budget (§4) = 500 MB/s minimum sequential read throughput required from replay storage, ruling out spinning disk (ADR-04).
  • C11 — Queue Drain Time at Shutdown. 100,000 events ÷ 5,000,000 events/s (C5 rate) = 20 ms drain time, inside the 3 s graceful-shutdown budget (§4) with 150x margin.
  • C12 — PMU Jitter Budget per Sample. ±2% variance tolerance (I6) × 500 ns p50 probe latency = ±10 ns jitter budget per sample, further tightened by C8's skew dominance.
