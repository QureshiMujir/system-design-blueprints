35. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. Page-table memory overhead per resident page. overhead ≈ 8 + 8/512 + 8/512² + 8/512³ bytes ≈ 8.06 B/page (one leaf PTE amortized with its fractional share of three upper levels, entries-per-table = 512 per §8.2). For a 4 GiB resident set (1,048,576 × 4 KiB pages): 1,048,576 × 8.06 B ≈ 8.45 MiB — about 0.2% overhead, chained from §4's 4 KiB base page size.
  2. TLB coverage, 4 KiB pages. coverage = entries × page_size. L1 DTLB: 64 × 4 KiB = 256 KiB. STLB: 1536 × 4 KiB = 6 MiB — both directly from §4's TLB-entry row.
  3. TLB coverage, 2 MiB huge pages. Assuming ~32 huge-page-capable TLB entries: 32 × 2 MiB = 64 MiB covered — a >10× improvement in address range covered per entry over item 2, the number that justifies THP (§23) for large working sets.
  4. IPIs avoided by batching a 1 GiB munmap. Naive: (region / page_size) × (N_cpus − 1) = (1 GiB / 4 KiB) × 511 = 262,144 × 511 ≈ 134,000,000 IPIs at the §4 512-CPU ceiling. Batched: 1 × 511 = 511 IPIs. Reduction factor: region / page_size = 262,144× — the headline number for P5/ADR-08, measured directly in §19.7.
  5. Buddy allocator max practical order. total_pages = physical_memory / page_size = 16 TiB / 4 KiB = 2^32 pages (§4's 16 TiB ceiling); the design caps representable single-block order at 11 (2^11 × 4 KiB = 8 MiB blocks) per ADR-04 — larger contiguous regions are represented as multiple order-11 blocks, a design choice traded off in §16.10, not a derived number.
  6. Zswap effective capacity. effective_capacity = pool_size × compression_ratio. With a 2 GiB RAM pool and the §4 target ratio of 2.5:1: 2 GiB × 2.5 = 5 GiB of anonymous pages held compressed before any disk-tier fallback — the number ADR-07 is justified by.
  7. Fault-handling throughput ceiling per core. rate = 1 / p50_latency = 1 / 3 µs ≈ 333,333 faults/sec/core (§4's anonymous-fault p50). Aggregate at the 512-CPU ceiling: rate × N_cpus ≈ 1.7 × 10^8 faults/sec system-wide theoretical ceiling, ignoring lock contention (a bound, not an achieved number).
  8. Page-fault queue memory at saturation. queue_mem = depth × N_cpus × record_size = 256 × 512 × 128 B ≈ 16.8 MiB total pending-fault bookkeeping (§4's 256-deep-per-core fault queue).
  9. VMA metadata memory ceiling per process. vma_mem = max_vmas × sizeof(VmAreaStruct) = 65,530 × 96 B ≈ 6.29 MiB worst case (§4's 65,530-VMA ceiling; 96 B is VmAreaStruct's §11 field-set estimate).
  10. Swap-tier latency ratio. disk_p99 / anon_p99 = 200 µs / 15 µs ≈ 13.3× (§4's SSD swap-in p99 vs. anonymous-fault p99) — the ratio that motivates zswap (item 6) absorbing the bulk of swap traffic to keep the system's overall fault-latency distribution close to the anonymous p99 rather than the disk-bound tail.
