32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — Device Table Footprint. 4,096 devices (§4) × 128 B per DeviceId+state metadata entry = 524,288 B ≈ 0.5 MB, negligible against the < 2% memory-overhead ceiling (§4).
  • C2 — Domain Table Footprint. 65,536 domains (§4) × 256 B DmaDomain header = 16,777,216 B = 16 MB, well inside a 2%-of-256 GB ceiling (5.12 GB).
  • C3 — IOVA/Mapping Index RAM. 50,000,000 mappings (§4) × 56 B index entry = 2,800,000,000 B = 2.8 GB, + 15% mapping-amplification slack (§4's 1.15× ceiling) = 3.22 GB, inside the 5.12 GB ceiling alongside C2's 16 MB.
  • C4 — FD/Handle Table. 1,000,000 FD budget (§4) × 40 B handle-table entry = 40,000,000 B = 40 MB, negligible against the ceiling.
  • C5 — Telemetry Ring Sizing. 2,000,000 events/sec sustained (§12.4) × 64 B record × 1 s burst window = 128,000,000 B = 128 MB ring, budgeted separately from the mapping index.
  • C6 — Invalidation Queue Memory. 16,384 queue depth (§4) × 32 B QueueInvalidationEntry × 16 NUMA-sharded queues (§4 NUMA node ceiling) = 8,388,608 B = 8 MB total across all shards.
  • C7 — Quarantine Grace-Period Backlog. 10,000 faults/sec sustained (§19.4) × 0.1 s completion-RTO window (§4) = 1,000 quarantined page-table nodes in flight, × 4 KB per-node slab overhead = 4,096,000 B ≈ 4 MB.
  • C8 — Availability Budget in Minutes. 0.0001 × 365 × 24 × 60 = 52.56 minutes/year unavailable (§4's 99.99% target), giving roughly 105 incidents/year at the < 30 s RTO (§4) before breaching the SLO.
