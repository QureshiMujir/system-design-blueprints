34. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

Step budget (from §4): 1000 ms ÷ 60 Hz = 16.667 ms per step.

Max contacts from solver cost: assume 60% of the step budget (10 ms = 10,000,000 ns) is allotted to the velocity + friction solve (§30's M1/M6/M7 split). Measured per-constraint cost (Phase 7 benchmark baseline, M6) ≈ 1,000 ns in the pure-Python core (ADR-02). With friction roughly doubling per-point cost (M7) and 8 velocity iterations (P1): effective cost per contact = 8 × 1,000 ns × 2 = 16,000 ns. Max contacts = 10,000,000 ÷ 16,000 ≈ 625 contacts — consistent with, and slightly above, §4's 500-contact sustained target (the margin absorbs position-solve and telemetry overhead).

Broad-phase crossover (naive vs. BVH): naive O(n²) cost ≈ n(n-1)/2 × 20 ns (a cheap AABB overlap test); BVH cost ≈ n·log2(n) × 150 ns (higher per-node constant from tree traversal in Python). Setting them equal: (n−1)×10 ≈ 150·log2(n). At n ≈ 100: log2(100) ≈ 6.64, RHS ≈ 996, LHS = 99×10 = 990 — approximately equal. Crossover n ≈ 100: below that, naive O(n²) is competitive; above it, BVH promotion (ADR-07) is mandatory, matching §4's "≤5,000 candidate pairs at 1,000-body density" framing (well past the crossover, BVH is the only viable structure).

Memory per step: 1,000 bodies × 512 B (M14) = 512,000 B (500 KB); 500 contacts × 256 B (M14) = 128,000 B (125 KB). Total live per-step state ≈ 625 KB — small enough that cache locality (SoA layout, ADR-02) matters far more than raw footprint.

Tunnelling threshold velocity: threshold = shape/wall thickness ÷ dt. For the §22 CCD fixture's 0.02 m wall at dt = 1/60 s (0.01667 s): threshold = 0.02 ÷ 0.01667 ≈ 1.2 m/s. Any relative approach velocity above this against that wall risks tunnelling in one discrete step — far below the fixture's dramatic 50 m/s test case, underscoring that CCD is mandatory for any fast body against thin geometry, not just bullets in the literal sense.

Penetration recovery rate (NGS position pass): with a per-iteration correction fraction β ≈ 0.2 and position_iterations = 3 (P1), depth after one step's position pass: depth × (1−β)³ = depth × 0.512. Starting from a 5 cm (0.05 m) initial overlap, reducing to below penetration_slop (0.002 m) requires 0.8ⁿ × 0.05 ≤ 0.002 → 0.8ⁿ ≤ 0.04 → n ≥ ln(0.04)/ln(0.8) ≈ 14.4 total iterations, i.e. ⌈14.4 / 3⌉ = 5 steps (≈ 83 ms at 60 Hz) to recover a 5 cm deep-overlap start to within tolerance.

Energy-drift estimate for semi-implicit Euler: per Hairer, Lubich & Wanner's quasi-symplectic bound, the per-step energy error oscillates rather than growing secularly, with amplitude scaling with dt but not accumulating monotonically. Phase 1's energy_drift_free_spin.json fixture measures this directly rather than relying on the bound alone; the §4/§13 target (≤1% drift per simulated second at dt = 1/60 s) is set with generous headroom above the quasi-symplectic bound's expected behavior, which is why I1's enforcement is a measured regression fixture, not a purely analytic guarantee.
