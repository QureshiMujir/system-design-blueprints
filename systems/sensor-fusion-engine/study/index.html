Mental Models & First Principles

# Model Expert insight What it predicts Bug class without it Applied in
MM1 Covariance is belief, not error P encodes what the filter claims to know; NIS checks if it matches reality. Filter consistency; NIS/NEES within χ² bounds. Overconfident P (underscaled Q): filter ignores valid measurements → slow divergence. §7 System Invariants (I10); Phase 9 (§21.2 NIS)
MM2 Error-state lives in the tangent space Attitude error δθ is a 3-vector in the Lie algebra; the nominal quaternion is the base point. Filtering happens in flat space; the manifold is only used at inject/reset. Linearization stays valid as long as δθ is small; reset returns error to zero. Filtering the full 4-vector creates a rank-deficient P and the norm constraint conflicts with the Kalman update. §8.2; Phase 7 (§19.2); ADR-5, ADR-7
MM3 Observability governs what the filter can learn A state is observable only if there exists a measurement sequence that produces non-zero Fisher information about it. Unobservable states (e.g. yaw without heading sensor) have bounded information; P in that direction should not shrink. Without FEJ, linearization drift manufactures spurious information → artificially confident yaw → catastrophic yaw bias. Phase 9 (§21.2 FEJ); §8.11 stationary case; ADR-30
MM4 Innovation is the signal; S is the noise The innovation y = z - h(x⁻) carries all new information. Its covariance S = HPHᵀ + R is the expected uncertainty. Mahalanobis distance normalizes the signal by the noise. A consistent filter's d² ~ χ²(dof). Persistent d² >> dof means model mismatch or outlier, not "large innovation". Gating without Mahalanobis (e.g. absolute threshold) rejects valid large-variance measurements and accepts normalized outliers. §8.5 update equations; Phase 9 (§21.2 mahalanobis.rs); I5, I10
MM5 Joseph form is insurance against numerical drift The standard (I-KH)P is algebraically equivalent to Joseph form but accumulates rounding error; Joseph form is SPD-preserving by construction. Over thousands of updates, standard form yields negative variances (NaN gain); Joseph does not. One non-Joseph update in 10⁴ steps causes an almost-undetectable slow drift toward non-PD covariance, then sudden filter death. Phase 4 (§16.2 joseph.rs); I3; P1
MM6 Time is a measurement, not a given Every sensor reading carries a timestamp that may be wrong, delayed, or out of order. The filter must treat the timestamp as input, not ground truth. Delayed/reordered measurements must be applied at their true time, not arrival time, to avoid correlated errors. Applying a GPS fix at arrival time (100 ms late) during acceleration introduces a position error that correlates with vehicle velocity — unmodeled and ungated. Phase 10 (§22.2 oosm.rs, latency.rs); I4; P11
MM7 Cholesky is both the gate and the guard Cholesky factorization of P and S serves double duty: it computes the matrix square root for UKF sigma points and signals non-PD before the update damages the state. A failing Cholesky on P or S is an early warning, not a catastrophic failure — the correct response is regularize and skip, not propagate NaN. Using explicit matrix inverse skips the PD check and produces NaN gain on the first ill-conditioned cycle. Phase 1 (§1.2 chol.rs); I3, I9; ADR-29; P8
MM8 Gravity is the reference; bias is what you estimate In the ESKF, gravity cancels exactly in the vertical channel at rest — any residual velocity is caused by accel bias. This makes a stationary vehicle a bias calibration fixture. Stationary velocity residual reveals b_a; gyro bias appears in attitude drift when turning. Without this understanding, engineers misattribute stationary drift to noise and inflate Q instead of estimating bias, destroying observability. §8.8 worked example; Phase 5 (§5.2 stationary test); I12

Glossary · 36 terms · site-wide glossary

NIS
Normalized Innovation Squared: yᵀS⁻¹y; expected ≈ measurement dof; online consistency metric.
NEES
Normalized Estimation Error Squared: (x−x̂)ᵀP⁻¹(x−x̂); expected ≈ state dof; consistency vs ground truth (test).
Innovation
Measurement residual y=z−h(x⁻).
Innovation covariance S
HPHᵀ+R; uncertainty of the innovation.
Mahalanobis distance
d²=yᵀS⁻¹y; χ²-distributed under correct model; basis of gating.
Strapdown mechanization
Integrating body-mounted IMU to propagate pose/velocity/attitude.
Lever arm
Vector offset of a sensor from the IMU/body origin (body frame).
FEJ
First-Estimates Jacobians: evaluate Jacobians at first estimate to preserve observability/consistency.
ZUPT
Zero-velocity update: pseudo-measurement v=0 when stationary.
Sigma points
Deterministic samples of a distribution used by the unscented transform.
Unscented transform
Propagate sigma points through nonlinearity, recombine to get mean/covariance.
ESKF
Error-State Kalman Filter: filter a small error state about an integrated nominal state.
MEKF
Multiplicative EKF: attitude error applied multiplicatively (quaternion).
Joseph form
Numerically stable covariance update (I−KH)P(I−KH)ᵀ+KRKᵀ.
SPD
Symmetric Positive-Definite (required of all covariances).
Quaternion (Hamilton)
Scalar-first unit quaternion, i j=k; used for attitude (body→nav).
Exp/Log map
Maps between SO(3) tangent (R³) and rotation group (quaternion/DCM).
DCM
Direction Cosine Matrix; rotation matrix R(q).
NED / ENU
North-East-Down / East-North-Up local tangent navigation frames.
ECEF
Earth-Centered Earth-Fixed Cartesian frame.
LLH
Latitude/Longitude/Height geodetic coordinates (WGS-84).
OOSM
Out-Of-Sequence Measurement: a measurement arriving after later-timestamped data.
Latency compensation
Applying a delayed measurement at its true timestamp (via rewind/clone).
Complementary filter
Frequency-domain blend of gyro (high-pass) and accel/mag (low-pass) for attitude.
Allan variance
Time-domain stability analysis yielding noise PSDs + bias instability.
Hard/soft iron
Magnetometer offset (hard) and scale/skew (soft) distortions.
Declination
Angle between magnetic and true north.
Coning/sculling
High-rate attitude/velocity integration corrections for rotating/vibrating motion.
Observability Gramian
Matrix whose rank indicates which states are estimable from the measurements.
Process noise Q
Covariance of model/integration uncertainty per step.
Measurement noise R
Covariance of sensor measurement noise.
Kalman gain K
PHᵀS⁻¹; weighting of innovation into the state correction.
MSCKF
Multi-State Constraint Kalman Filter (visual-inertial; deferred).
ATE / RPE
Absolute Trajectory Error / Relative Pose Error (trajectory evaluation metrics).
Dead reckoning
Propagating state from IMU alone without aiding corrections.
Tightly/loosely coupled
INS/GNSS fusion at raw-measurement vs computed-solution level.
