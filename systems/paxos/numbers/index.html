34. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

Every number below is derived from the System Constraints table (§4); none is asserted without its formula. Assumes n=5 (quorum=3) and M1's workload profile (1KB values, batch=64, pipeline=16) unless stated otherwise.

  1. Quorum size. quorum = ⌊n/2⌋ + 1. For n=5: ⌊5/2⌋+1 = 3 (§4 row 2).
  2. Messages per decision, steady state. 1 round (§4 row 4) × (n-1) Accept sends + (n-1) Accepted replies = 2×(n-1) = 8 messages for n=5.
  3. Leader outbound bandwidth at M1. throughput × avg_msg_size × fanout = 100,000/s × 1,088 B × (n-1=2, at the n=3 localhost bench in §4 row 5) ≈ 217.6 MB/s.
  4. WAL growth rate at M1. throughput × record_size = 100,000/s × 1,052 B (12B ballot + 8B slot + 8B header + 1,024B value) ≈ 105.2 MB/s raw write rate before compaction.
  5. Snapshot cadence at M1. trigger_size ÷ growth_rate = 64 MB ÷ 105.2 MB/s ≈ 0.61s per snapshot at full M1 throughput (ADR-14) — over a 1M-write soak (§17.3) this predicts 1,052,000,000 B ÷ 64,000,000 B ≈ 16.4 snapshots, consistent with the "≤ 20 snapshots" acceptance criterion in §17.4.
  6. In-flight pipeline memory. window × msg_size × peers = 16 × 1,052 B × 4 ≈ 67.3 KB per leader for n=5 — trivial next to the 256 MB ceiling (§4 row 17), showing the ceiling is dominated by log/snapshot memory, not pipeline buffers.
  7. Steady-state log-memory headroom. Bounded by the snapshot trigger (64 MB) plus one in-flight batch (64 × 1,052 B ≈ 67 KB) ≈ 64.07 MB steady state versus the 256 MB ceiling (§4 row 17) — ~4× headroom for burst absorption before the ceiling is at risk.
  8. FD budget at n=5. (n-1) peer sockets + 1 WAL fd + 1 snapshot fd = 6 FDs; during a reconfiguration (§18, transient double-connections) this roughly doubles to 12 FDs — still well under the 32-FD budget (§4 row 18).
  9. Storage amplification, steady state. (snapshot_size + retention_window × record_size) ÷ logical_size. For 1M keys at ~1KB logical average (~1 GB logical data), the 16-entry retention window (ADR-15) contributes 16 × 1,052 B ≈ 16.8 KB — negligible — giving ≈1.0–1.05× amplification steady state, well under the ≤2× budget (§4 row 23); the 2× budget covers the transient window where two full snapshots briefly coexist before GC.
  10. Reconfiguration overlap window, logical time. α ÷ throughput = 3 ÷ 100,000/s = 30 microseconds of logical slot-time at M1 — the 10-second wall-clock completion target (M12) is therefore dominated by the new node's snapshot catch-up time (M10: ≤1s for 64MB), not by the α-window itself.
  11. WAN throughput ceiling via batching. At WAN RTT=30ms (§4 row 19) and 1 round/decision steady-state (§4 row 4), one unpipelined proposer caps at 1 ÷ 0.030s ≈ 33 decisions/sec. Reaching M3 (≥5,000 commits/sec) needs pipeline_window × batch_size ÷ RTT = 16 × 64 ÷ 0.030s ≈ 34,133 values/sec theoretical ceiling — comfortably covering M3 with headroom, confirming batching (ADR-12), not raw pipeline depth, is the WAN lever.
