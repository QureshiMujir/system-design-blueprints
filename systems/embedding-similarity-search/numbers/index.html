34. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

Every number below is derived from §4's System Constraints table.

  1. RAM for 100M vectors, dim=768, f32, uncompressed: 100,000,000 × 768 × 4 bytes = 307.2 GB — exceeds the §4 64 GB single-shard ceiling by 4.8×, forcing either quantization or sharding.
  2. HNSW graph overhead at M=16, same corpus: 100,000,000 × 16 × 2 (bidirectional) × 8 bytes (id + weight) ≈ 25.6 GB — added to (1), total ≈ 332.8 GB uncompressed.
  3. Shard count needed for (1)+(2) uncompressed: 332.8 GB ÷ 64 GB/shard ≈ 6 shards minimum (rounds to 8 for headroom), directly exercising §21's sharding phase.
  4. RAM with IVF-PQ (m=64, ks=8) instead of raw f32: compressed vector = 64 bytes/vector; 100,000,000 × 64 bytes = 6.4 GB + graph-equivalent IVF list overhead (~100,000,000 × 4 bytes cluster-id ≈ 0.4 GB) ≈ 6.8 GB total — fits in a single §4 shard with room for 9× more vectors before hitting the ceiling.
  5. QPS achievable per shard at the §4 P50 = 2 ms: 1000 ms ÷ 2 ms × 256 concurrent queries (§4 concurrency ceiling) ≈ 128,000 theoretical max, bounded in practice by the stated 20,000 QPS/shard sustained target (§4) once contention and tail effects are included.
  6. Network bandwidth for a 16-shard fan-out at the sustained 20,000 QPS/shard: query payload ≈ 768 × 4 bytes = 3,072 bytes; 16 shards × 20,000 QPS × 3,072 bytes ≈ 983 MB/s aggregate fan-out traffic — within a 10 GbE (1.25 GB/s) link but worth tracking per §26 dashboards.
  7. FD budget check: §4's 4,096 FDs/process against one mmap segment per index shard plus O(concurrency) control sockets: 256 concurrent queries × 1 socket + ~10 mmap segments ≈ 266 FDs — well within budget, headroom for 15× concurrency growth before hitting the ceiling.
  8. Build time for 100M vectors at the §4/§17.4 50,000 vectors/s HNSW build rate: 100,000,000 ÷ 50,000 = 2,000 s ≈ 33.3 minutes per shard-worth of vectors — informs the resharding runbook's expected downtime window (§26).
  9. Storage amplification check (§4: ≤ 1.3×): a 6.8 GB in-memory IVF-PQ shard (item
    1. serializes to at most 6.8 GB × 1.3 = 8.84 GB on disk — bounds the backup storage budget in §26.
  10. Cross-shard merge overhead at 16 shards (§21.4 target ≤ 1.5 ms added P99): 16 shards × ~0.1 ms per-shard result-collection overhead ≈ 1.6 ms raw, brought under the 1.5 ms target via parallel (not sequential) shard-result collection — the number that motivates §21.2's fan-out-not-sequential design.
