36. Alternative Architectures (Roads Not Taken)

(a) GAN-based single-step generation

z ~ N(0,I) ---> [ Generator G ] ---> image
                       ^
                       | adversarial signal
      image (real/fake) <---[ Discriminator D ]

Winning regime: real-time-adjacent single-step generation with no multi-step sampler at all. Embodying system: Karras et al.'s StyleGAN2/3. Rejected here: no ELBO/likelihood objective (§8 has no GAN analogue), well-documented training instability (mode collapse) versus diffusion's stable MSE-regression training (P5), and no natural home for the schedule/preconditioning abstraction (P1) this blueprint's entire capability arc is built around.

(b) Autoregressive discrete-token modeling (VQGAN + Transformer)

image -> [VQ Encoder] -> discrete tokens -> [causal Transformer] -> logits -> [VQ Decoder] -> image

Winning regime: exact likelihood, simple prompt-token-concatenation conditioning, strong compositional generation. Embodying system: Esser, Rombach & Ommer's VQGAN+Transformer (Taming Transformers, CVPR 2021, §5 paper 17); the original DALL-E. Rejected here: sampling is O(sequence length) sequential, not parallelizable the way a diffusion denoiser's O(NFE) is (breaks §4's sampling-latency story); discrete codebook collapse is a hard failure mode this design's continuous KL-latent (ADR-11) avoids by construction.

(c) Flow matching / rectified flow instead of the diffusion SDE

x_0 (data) ---straight-line ODE path---> x_1 (noise)
   learn velocity field v_θ(x_t,t) = x_1 - x_0  (no schedule/preconditioning bookkeeping)

Winning regime: simpler training objective, no terminal-SNR concept to get wrong, fewer numerically-tricky corners. Embodying system: Lipman et al.'s Flow Matching; Liu et al.'s Rectified Flow; Stable Diffusion 3's backbone. Rejected here: this blueprint's Phase 0–5 arc (§8's diffusion SDE, EDM preconditioning, zero-SNR fix) is built around the score/diffusion formulation specifically because it has the deepest catalogue of existing reference checkpoints for differential testing (§1 language rationale, ADR-02) — flow matching is a legitimate alternative foundation, not a parameter swap on top of this one.

(d) Consistency models trained directly for few-step sampling

x_t (any noise level) ---[ consistency function f_θ ]---> x_0, in one step
   trained for self-consistency along the PF-ODE trajectory, not distilled post-hoc

Winning regime: single-step or few-step (2–4 NFE) generation trained end-to-end for that regime from the start. Embodying system: Song, Dhariwal, Chen & Sutskever's Consistency Models (2023). Rejected here: Phase 9 explicitly chooses progressive distillation (Salimans & Ho 2022, P11) targeting a teacher trajectory rather than an invented self-consistency objective (P11's explicit rule); consistency models are this blueprint's documented next step past its ~10-NFE floor (§21 N.6) — the PhaseDeferred single-step-training item in §37, not built here because P11 restricts this phase to trajectory-matching distillation only.
