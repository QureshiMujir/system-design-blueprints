4.1 Worked complexity derivation

n rows × p features:

  • Sorted-scan split: presort each feature (O(n log n)); one L→R scan updating left/right class counts → O(1)/threshold → O(p·n log n) per level, ×depth per tree (presort amortizes the log).
  • Histogram split: pre-bin each feature into B bins once (O(n·p)); per node accumulate per-bin grad/hess (O(n·p)), scan B thresholds (O(p·B)) → O(n·p + p·B) per node, scan independent of n → ≥5× win at large n (B≈256 ≪ n).
  • GBDT: M trees × per-tree; grad/hess O(n)/stage. Shrinkage η trades trees for accuracy.
  • SMO: each step O(n) (two cached kernel rows); convergence empirical, bounded by max_iter; max-KKT-violation pair selection accelerates.
  • k-means: Lloyd iter O(n·k·p) assign + O(n·p) update; ×n_init restarts.
  • PCA: center O(n·p); SVD O(min(n,p)·n·p) (#349) — dominant. Constants come from #349 GEMM/SVD + cache behavior.

36. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

System Constraints (from §4): dense f64 baseline; n up to 10⁶, p up to 10⁴ dense; fits in RAM; x86-64 with AVX2.

C1. Feature matrix memory (dense, f64) Formula: mem = n × p × 8 bytes At n=10⁶, p=10³: 10⁶ × 10³ × 8 = 8 GB — just within a 16 GB server; p=10⁴ → 80 GB (needs batching or sparse). At n=10⁵, p=10³: 10⁵ × 10³ × 8 = 800 MB — comfortable for training.

C2. Sorted split-finding time per tree (exact) Formula: T_exact = depth × p × n × C_compare; C_compare ≈ 3 ns (branch + increment + compare). At depth=10, p=100, n=10⁵: 10 × 100 × 10⁵ × 3ns = 3 s/tree — slow; histogram needed. At depth=10, p=100, n=10³: 10 × 100 × 10³ × 3ns = 3 ms/tree — fine.

C3. Histogram split-finding time per tree Formula: T_hist = depth × (p × n × C_accum + p × B × C_scan); C_accum ≈ 1 ns/sample (sequential write), C_scan ≈ 0.5 ns/bin. At depth=10, p=100, n=10⁵, B=256: 10 × (100 × 10⁵ × 1ns + 100 × 256 × 0.5ns) ≈ 100ms/tree. Speedup vs C2: 3s/100ms = 30× (exceeds the ≥5× §4 target at this n).

C4. GBDT training time (M stages) Formula: T_gbdt = M × T_hist + M × n × C_grad; C_grad ≈ 5 ns/sample (sigmoid + grad). At M=100, n=10⁵: 100 × 100ms + 100 × 10⁵ × 5ns = 10s + 0.05s ≈ 10s — feasible. At M=1000, n=10⁶: 1000 × 1s + 1000 × 10⁶ × 5ns ≈ 1000s + 5s ≈ 17 min — large run.

C5. Gram matrix memory (SVM, dense) Formula: mem_gram = n² × 8 bytes At n=10⁴: 10⁸ × 8 = 800 MB — borderline; n=3×10⁴ → 7.2 GB (hard ceiling → kernel cache). Cache budget (ADR-13): 200 MB → n_cached = 200MB / (n × 8B) = 200MB / 80kB = 2500 rows at n=10⁴.

C6. SVM predict time (n_sv support vectors) Formula: T_predict_per_row = n_sv × C_kernel; C_kernel(RBF) ≈ 2p flops ≈ 5 ns at p=100 with SIMD. At n_sv=1000, p=100: 1000 × 5ns = 5 µs/row — acceptable for offline; tight for 10⁴ rows/s online. At n_sv=10⁴, p=100: 10⁴ × 5ns = 50 µs/row — SVM predict bottleneck; use linear kernel or kernel approx §24.2.

C7. k-means assign time per iteration Formula: T_assign = n × k × p × C_dist / (GEMM_efficiency); GEMM: ‖x−μ‖²=‖x‖²−2Xμᵀ+‖μ‖²; throughput ≈ peak GEMM / (n×k×p) flops. At n=10⁵, k=100, p=50: 10⁵ × 100 × 50 × 2 flops = 10⁹ flops; AVX2 GEMM at 30 GFLOPS → 33 ms/iter. 300 iters × 33 ms = 10 s total — feasible.

C8. PCA SVD time Formula: T_svd = O(min(n,p) × n × p) flops; at n=10⁵, p=100, min=100: 100 × 10⁵ × 100 = 10⁹ flops; AVX2 → 33 ms — fast. At n=10³, p=10⁴, min=10³: 10³ × 10³ × 10⁴ = 10¹⁰ flops → 333 ms; covariance route p×p eigen cheaper: 10⁴ × 10³ × 10⁴ + 10¹²... no, use SVD thin (n×p) path via #349 truncated.

C9. Random forest parallel scaling Formula: T_rf = n_estimators × T_tree / n_jobs (trivially parallel, I11). At n_estimators=100, T_tree=100ms, n_jobs=8: 100 × 100ms / 8 = 1.25s — 8× speedup = linear (§4 ≥0.85× target, reached). Memory: 100 trees × 10-depth × 2^10 nodes × ~100 bytes/node = 10 MB — small.

C10. Tree predict latency Formula: T_predict_per_row = depth × C_branch; C_branch ≈ 3 ns (index lookup + compare + branch, SoA cache-friendly). At depth=20: 20 × 3ns = 60 ns/row — well under §4 target of 1 µs/row. 10⁶ rows → 60 ms.

C11. Histogram buffer memory Formula: mem_hist = n_features × n_bins × 3 × 8 bytes (grad_sum, hess_sum, count as f64). At p=1000, B=256: 1000 × 256 × 3 × 8 = 6 MB per node; with two-node look-ahead: 12 MB — small. Pool reuse means this is a fixed allocation for the duration of training (principle 12, P12).

C12. Cross-validation wall time (grid search) Formula: T_cv = |grid| × k_folds × T_fit_single At |grid|=50, k=5, T_fit=10s: 50 × 5 × 10 = 2500s ≈ 42 min — need parallelism or randomized search. At n_jobs=8: 2500/8 = 312s ≈ 5 min — tolerable; use RandomizedSearchCV to subsample the grid.
