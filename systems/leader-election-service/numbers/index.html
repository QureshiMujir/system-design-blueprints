4. System Constraints

The defining property: safety is never traded for availability — under any partition the service keeps at most one leader per group even if that means the minority (and sometimes the whole cluster) rejects writes (P1, MM3). 32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

Each number is derived from §4 (C#) with the formula shown; nothing asserted.

  1. Safe lease TTL floor (→ C15). TTL ≥ 2·RTT + fdatasync + drift + margin. From C2 (RTT p99 20 ms), C5 (fdatasync 3 ms), C16 (drift 100 ms): 2·20 + 3 + 100 = 143 ms; add ~40% margin ⇒ ≥ 250 ms = C15. This is why a client RenewDeadline below this flaps (FM-8, §17).
  2. Leader→follower heartbeat rate. (n-1) · (1000/interval). From C1 (n=7) and C18 (100 ms): 6 · 10 = 60 msgs/s; at ~256 B ⇒ 60·256 = 15.4 KB/s — negligible (justifies M9 < 5%).
  3. Replication bandwidth at peak. throughput · entry_size · (n-1). From C3 (25,000/s), 256 B entry, n=7: 25,000 · 256 · 6 = 38.4 MB/s outbound on the leader — fits a 10 GbE NIC with headroom.
  4. WAL write rate & amplification. throughput · entry_size · amplification. From C3, 256 B, C19 (1.5×): 25,000 · 256 · 1.5 = 9.6 MB/s fsync-backed — trivial for NVMe (justifies M3).
  5. Group-commit batch size needed. throughput · fdatasync_p99. From C3 and C5 (3 ms): 25,000 · 0.003 = 75 entries/fsync minimum batch to keep fsync off the critical path (justifies ADR-07 group commit).
  6. Watch memory at fan-out (→ C7/M10). watchers · per_watch_bytes. From C6 (200,000) and per-watch ≈ 4 KB (cursor 16 B + bounded queue 32×64 B + gRPC/task overhead ~2 KB): 200,000 · 4 KB = 800 MB — under C7 (8 GiB) with room for state/log.
  7. FD usage at fan-out (→ C13). watchers/streams_per_conn + peers + listeners. From C6, ~100 multiplexed streams/conn (ADR-05), C1: 200,000/100 + 6 + few ≈ 2,010 FDs — far under C13 (65,536).
  8. Cold replay time (→ C8/M6). entries / apply_rate. Apply is in-memory map insert ≈ 1M/s; 1,000,000 / 1,000,000 ≈ 1 s CPU + IO read of ~256 MB at NVMe 2 GB/s ≈ 0.13 s ⇒ well under C8 (15 s), leaving margin for CRC verification.
  9. Snapshot recovery (→ C9/M7). snapshot_bytes / (net_or_disk_rate). 1 GiB over 10 GbE ≈ 1 GiB / 1.25 GB/s ≈ 0.9 s transfer + deserialize ≈ few s ⇒ under C9 (60 s) with wide margin.
  10. Failover time (→ C11/M5). detection + election + first_commit. From C18: detection ≈ 1 election timeout (~225 ms avg), election ≈ 1–2 timeouts (150–600 ms), first commit ≈ RTT+fsync (~23 ms) ⇒ typically < 1 s, worst case ≤ 3 timeouts (M13) — inside C11 (60 s) and M5 (2 s).
  11. In-RAM Raft log before snapshot. retained_entries · entry_size. Keeping 100,000 recent entries: 100,000 · 256 B = 25.6 MB — trivial vs C7; triggers a snapshot to cap it.
  12. Max independent groups per node (memory-bound). (C7 − overhead) / per_group_state. With ~1 GiB reserved for watches/log and per-group ElectionRecord ≈ 128 B: ~6 GiB / 128 B ≈ 50M groups theoretical; practically bounded by watch/session memory (#6), so groups scale by sharding (§29), not per-node stuffing.
