The one defining property: soundness has a fixed, non-negotiable latency budget — every one of the numbers above exists so that "correct" and "fast enough to run on every commit" are the same requirement, not a tradeoff made per release.

34. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • CN1 — Serial per-function pipeline latency. ΣM1..M6 p50 = 10 + 50 + 100 + 200 + 100 + 100 = 560 ms/function (§30).
  • CN2 — Parallel throughput ceiling. cores × (1 / serial_latency) × (1 − overhead) = 16 × (1 / 0.560) × 0.85 ≈ 24 functions/sec on the reference HW (§4), with a 15% derate for rayon pool overhead (ADR-20).
  • CN3 — Corpus check time (consistency check against M8). corpus_size / throughput = 1,000 / 24 ≈ 42 s — within the M8 p99 target of 45 s (§30), confirming CN1/CN2's derivation chain.
  • CN4 — Constraint-set size per function. statements × constraints_per_stmt × bytes_per_constraint = 1,000 × 2 × 64 B ≈ 128 KiB per 1,000-statement function's ConstraintSet (§15.7).
  • CN5 — Concurrent constraint-set memory. per_function_footprint × concurrency_degree = 128 KiB × 16 ≈ 2 MiB resident for constraint sets across the full worker pool (ADR-20) — small relative to the §4 2 GiB ceiling, meaning region-graph memory is not the dominant cost at this corpus scale.
  • CN6 — Diagnostic JSON report size. diagnostics_per_function × bytes_per_diagnostic × function_count = 5 × 400 B × 1,000 = 2 MB for an adversarial 5-rejections-per-function corpus — within the §4 5 MiB report ceiling.
  • CN7 — Worst-case single-region size. points_per_function × bytes_per_point = 2,000 × 16 B ≈ 32 KiB for one pathological region bounded by I6's cap on a 1,000-statement function.
  • CN8 — FD headroom. fixed_fds + worker_temp_fds ≈ 1 (input) + 1 (output) + 3 (stdio) = 5 FDs against the §4 cap of 64 — 12.8× headroom.
  • CN9 — Cold-start budget. crates × per_crate_init_cost = 13 × ~2 ms ≈ 26 ms, leaving 24 ms headroom before the M14/§4 50 ms ceiling.
  • CN10 — Fuzz canary fleet throughput. exec_rate × seconds_per_day × target_count = 10,000 × 86,400 × 4 ≈ 3.46 billion executions/day fleet-wide across the four ADR-12 targets during the M11 24-hour canary.

Every number above chains from §4's System Constraints table and reappears unchanged in §30's M-table or §23's phase acceptance criteria (H13's "derived, never asserted" bar).
