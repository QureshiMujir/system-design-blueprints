32. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  • C1 — TAGE table RAM. 12 tables × 2^16 entries × 5 B/TageEntry = 3,932,160 B ≈ 3.75 MB per table set × 4 concurrent geometric-length configs ≈ 15 MB, inside the 256 MB §4 predictor-table ceiling.
  • C2 — Perceptron table RAM. 2^16 PC-indexed entries × 64-weight vector × 2 B/weight = 8,388,608 B ≈ 8 MB, + 2^16 × 8 B metadata ≈ 524 KB, total ≈ 8.5 MB, leaving 232.5 MB of the 256 MB ceiling.
  • C3 — Replay buffer sizing. 64 MB ÷ 96 B/PredictorContext = 699,050 in-flight contexts supportable, versus a realistic max speculative depth of ~4,096 (I13-bounded) = 170× headroom.
  • C4 — Trace window vs. 10 GB trace. 512 MB window ÷ 10 B/BranchEvent = 53.7M events resident per window; 10 GB trace ÷ ~10 B/event ≈ 1.07B events ÷ 53.7M events/window ≈ 20 window rotations.
  • C5 — Metrics buffer under high cardinality. 32 MB ÷ 3.2 KB/histogram = 10,000 distinct label-set histograms supportable, matching the §4-stated 10K cardinality design point exactly.
  • C6 — Total RSS check. 15 MB (C1) + 8.5 MB (C2) + 64 MB (replay) + 512 MB (trace window) + 32 MB (metrics) + 128 MB (checkpoint cache) + 500 MB (runtime/thread-stack overhead) = 1,259.5 MB, inside the < 1.5 GB §4 ceiling.
  • C7 — Throughput headroom. 100M events/sec (M2 replay-scheduling target) ÷ 50M events/sec (M1 branch-event floor) = 2.0×, provisioning the scheduler at 2× the ingest floor.
  • C8 — FD budget under max concurrency. 256 worker threads (§4) ÷ 64-FD trace budget (§4) = 4 threads/FD average, forcing FD reuse via windowed round-robin rather than raw thread-count sizing.
  • C9 — Predictor-lookup latency vs. throughput consistency. 1 ÷ 50,000,000 events/sec (M1) = 20 ns/event mean inter-event budget = exactly the p99 lookup target (M5, < 20 ns), so M5 consumes the entire per-event budget at the tail.
  • C10 — Checkpoint flush time vs. cache ceiling. 128 MB checkpoint cache ÷ 1 GB/sec (M6 floor) = 0.128 s = 128 ms worst-case full-cache flush, inside the < 5 s checkpoint-restore target (M8).
