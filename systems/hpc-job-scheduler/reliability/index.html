Invariants — what must never be false (§7) · 7

Invariants — what must never be false
AttributeDetail
StatementEvery allocatable unit (core / GPU / mem byte) is assigned to at most one running job at any instant
Why it mattersPrevents resource contention and correctness violations in running workloads; I1 is the primary safety guarantee
Enforced inhpcsched-alloc: compare-and-set on CoreBitmap/GpuSet before WAL commit; hpcsched-state: WAL record integrity
DetectionIntegrity scan at startup and after failover: sum all live Allocation bitmaps per node; assert pairwise disjoint
Telemetryallocation_conflict_total counter — must be 0 in production; alert on any non-zero value
RecoveryHalt new scheduling on the affected node; alert ops; trigger controlled failover for manual bitmap audit
DebuggingReplay WAL to conflict timestamp; diff CoreBitmap at each JobAllocated record; identify overlapping commit sequence
