35. Back-of-Envelope Capacity Model

  1. Storage overhead, RS(6,3) = (k+m)/k = 9/6 = 1.50× (50% overhead), chained from §4's storage-amplification row.
  2. Storage overhead, RS(17,3) (Backblaze shape) = 20/17 ≈ 1.176× (17.6% overhead), same formula, chained from §4/§5 (Backblaze reference).
  3. Fleet scrub bandwidth = fleet_bytes / cycle_seconds = 4×10^15 B / (14×86400 s) ≈ 3.3 GB/s, chained from §4's scrub-budget row (M9, §31).
  4. Pure-RS single-shard repair bandwidth (4 MiB chunk, k=6) = k × chunk_size = 6 × 4 MiB = 24 MiB per repaired chunk, chained from §4's chunk-size assumption and §19's planner accounting.
  5. Repair time at 10 Gbps fabric = 24 MiB / 1.25 GB/s ≈ 19.2 ms per chunk, chained from item 4 and §4's network-RTT/fabric assumption.
  6. LRC (12,2,2) single-shard repair bandwidth = r × chunk_size = 6 × 4 MiB = 24 MiB → 12 MiB relative to a 12-data-shard pure-RS equivalent, i.e. a 2× reduction, chained from §21's r=6 vs. k=12 (M10, §31).
  7. SIMD speedup, AVX2 vs. scalar = 6.0 GB/s / 1.0 GB/s = 6×, chained from §4 (M1, M2).
  8. SIMD speedup, GFNI vs. scalar = 15.0 GB/s / 1.0 GB/s = 15×, chained from §4 (M1, M3).
  9. Working memory per concurrent encode = n × chunk_size = 9 × 4 MiB = 36 MiB; at 64 concurrent encodes (§4's concurrency ceiling), 64 × 36 MiB ≈ 2.3 GiB, chained from §4's memory-ceiling and max-concurrency rows.
  10. Max field-bound shard count = 2^8 = 256 elements, giving k+m ≤ 255 strict / 256 convention, chained from §4/§9 ADR-03 (P1, I7).
  11. Scrubber FD budget = concurrency × n = 4 × 9 = 36 FDs, well under a 1024 default ulimit, chained from §4's FD-budget row.
  12. Hitchhiker repair-bandwidth reduction = 25–45% applied to item 4's 24 MiB baseline, i.e. 13.2–18 MiB per repaired chunk, chained from §22's measured Facebook warehouse-cluster figures (Rashmi 2014, §5).
