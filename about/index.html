About this site

The 418 engineering blueprints are locked, self-contained specifications for building real systems from scratch — a rate limiter, a storage engine, a compiler, a consensus protocol — each between roughly 1,800 and 5,000 lines. Every blueprint has the same skeleton: goal and scope, why it is worth building, an architectural overview with the system's own ASCII diagram, numeric constraints, required reading, design principles, invariants, the domain model with its equations and state machines, a tech-stack decision table that records what was chosen, what was rejected and when the rejected choice would be right, a repository layout, core type signatures, ten to fourteen build phases with acceptance criteria, a threat model, failure modes and recovery, performance targets, a comparison with real systems, a capacity model, hard questions, roads not taken, a deferred-features register, and a glossary. This site is generated from those documents and from a structured extraction of them; nothing here is written by hand except the plain-words explainers.

How to read a system page. Each system opens on its Explainer — the plain-words account of why the system exists, how one unit of work flows through it, the handful of decisions where good engineers disagree, what breaks in production, and the rough numbers. Citations in the explainer such as §8.6, ADR-10, I2 or FM-3 are links to the exact table row or section they come from. Where an explainer has not been written yet, the page opens on the architecture overview instead and says so. The remaining tabs are generated for every system and are always one click away: the tab strip under the title lists them, and each has its own URL so you can link straight to a trade-off card, an invariant row or a build phase. Numbers on the Numbers tab are the blueprint's design targets, not measurements, unless a build status says otherwise. The badges under the title show whether the explainer is a draft or has been checked against the blueprint, and how far an actual build has got.

Where the data comes from

  • blueprints/index/INDEX.json — the catalogue (domain, language, environment, size, phase count).
  • blueprints/index/<slug>.json — the structured extraction behind every generated tab.
  • blueprints/<name>.md — the blueprint itself; Architecture, Numbers, Study and Blueprint tabs render slices of it.
  • blueprints/explainers/<slug>.md — the plain-words explainer, when written.
  • systems/<slug>/STATUS.json — the live build status badge.
